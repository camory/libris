cover, as `v0.10.0` declares; the backend pins that release. The frontend
stopped reading `coverUrl` in T060 and is deployed with T063, so the field
can leave without breaking the pair running.

## Risks and decisions
**The pin bump is a guard, its mutation run after step 2.** The pin alone
reds nothing, since the verifier refuses an extra field only where a schema
says `additionalProperties: false`, which `IsbnLookup` cannot (it is
composed over `Edition`). What proves the pin is that the old release now
refuses the answer: the mutation needs step 2's green, as T063's step 2
guard ran after its step 4. Rejected: the pin bump as part of step 2's green
(D07 opens a task with the pin bump); no guard on the pin (a pin set back
would go unnoticed).

**S5's line is deleted in step 2, not in a step of its own.** Its deletion
has no red of its own and is forced by step 2's green; a separate step
would be neither red first nor a guard. Rejected: deleting it before step 2
(a test change with no red).
