# T060 — Covers on `v0.9.0`, the card shows the first candidate

**Backlog line:** Frontend: covers on `v0.9.0`, the card shows the first
candidate; the clients tell problems apart by HTTP status (404, 503, 400);
the add sends `coverSource` null until T062. Realises S1, S11. Full text in
`agent/TASKS.md`.

**Serves:** spec `covers` S1 and S11 on the frontend; PRD §4.1 and §4.7; D04,
D05, D07, D10, D11; U06, U08. The precondition is met: T059 (#201) is merged,
#202 put `id` and `covers` into the Givens, and #203 stubs
`IntersectionObserver` in `CoversScenarios.open()`.

**First of its kind:** the first client that reads a problem from the **HTTP
status** instead of the body's `type`, and the first card prop that is a **list
of candidates** rather than a field of the edition.

## Goal
The frontend speaks `v0.9.0`: the lookup's card shows the first cover the
sources offer, or the stand-in, and the catalogue rows Libris's own cover.
T061, T062 and T063 then build on the card's `covers`.

## Risks and decisions
**The card takes the whole list, `covers: CoverCandidate[]`, and shows the
first.** T061 chooses the first candidate that loads and draws a dot per
candidate, both inside the card. Handing the card the list now means T061
changes one component and not its caller.
- Rejected: the view passes one `coverUrl`. T061 would change the prop again.
- Rejected: the card keeps reading `edition.coverUrl`. `v0.9.0`'s `Edition`
  has no such field.

**`SourceEdition` loses `coverUrl`, and the client ignores the lookup's
deprecated `coverUrl`.** The edition of `v0.9.0` has none, and `v0.10.0` drops
the lookup's field, so reading it now would be read for one release only. The
removal is step 11's refactor and not a cycle of its own: no behaviour changes
once the card reads `covers`.
- Rejected: keeping the field filled from `covers[0]`. That duplicates the
  prop and misleads T063.

**The clients map the status, never the body, for the statuses the contract
names.** `v0.9.0` gives a problem's `type` no value, and the mock answers a
random one (probed while planning).
- `FetchIsbnApi`: 404 → `/problems/not-found`, 503 →
  `/problems/sources-unavailable`, 400 → `/problems/validation`.
- `FetchBookshelfApi`: 400 and 404 the same way.
- Any other status keeps today's reading of the body's `type`. That is no new
  code, and `IsbnView` already falls back to *isbn.error* for a type it does not
  know.
- The answers keep `{outcome: "problem", type}`, as every fake builds them.
- Rejected: a `status` field on the outcome. That changes the port and every
  fake for nothing a screen reads.

**The add forwards the port's third parameter now, and sends `null` when it
is absent.**
- `NewBook` requires `coverSource`: the mock refuses a body without it.
- The keyed examples (201, 400 wrong digit, 404 foreign bookshelf) match only
  the body carrying `"inventaire.io"`. A `null` gets a generated 201 on both
  ids (probed). So the mock cases pass `"inventaire.io"`, which also proves the
  value is forwarded.
- `useAddBookToBookshelf` and `IsbnView` pass nothing until T062.
- Rejected: always sending `null`. The mock's 400 and 404 would then be
  unreachable.

**S11 is un-skipped as a guard**: `CatalogueRow` already shows
`Book.coverUrl`, and #203 made the case runnable.
