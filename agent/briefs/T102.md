## Goal
`GET /api/v1/series/{id}` answers the series of the reader's catalogue
with the series list's summary of it and its editions on the reader's
bookshelves, each with its copies there; a series outside their catalogue
stays no such series. The backend then serves the whole page, and T103
moves the frontend once this is deployed.

## Rules in play
- D02 — the read lives in `FindCatalogueSeries`, in
  `library.application.catalogue`; the controller, its responses and the
  contract test stay as T101 left them.
- D07 — the scenario `S1 The reader opens a series` is un-skipped and
  nothing else of `SeriesPageScenarios` changes but the now unused
  `Disabled` import; the use case is proven by plain JUnit over the
  fakes of its ports; no web test; `ApiContractTest` runs the same cases
  as on `main`; each guard's mutation run, reverted, listed in the PR.
- D10 — `FindCatalogueSeries` keeps `operator fun invoke` as its one
  public member and loses its `@Suppress("UnusedParameter")`; it calls
  `ListCatalogueSeries`, the public door to the summary, whose private
  `seriesOf` stays private; the variables are `listCatalogueSeries`,
  `bookshelves`, `copies`, `editions`, `seriesRepository`, as the
  neighbouring use cases name them; no rationale and no `D##` in code.
- D11 — no such series is the result `NoSuchSeries`, never an exception;
  the books come in no promised order and no Kotlin code sorts them.
- D12 — each aggregate is read through its own repository and no
  repository joins another; the name a book's series entry carries is
  read through `SeriesRepository` and entered by
  `SeriesVolume.entryIn(series)`; `Found` stays a data class with no rule.
- PRD §3 — an edition is an `Edition`, a series a `Series`; "book" names
  only the contract's `Book` and the `books` of `Found`.

2. **Red.** *a series of the reader's catalogue is found with what the

3. **Red.** *the series answers its editions on the reader's bookshelves,

   book per copy.
4. **Red** if step 3's green answered one book per copy; otherwise a

5. **Red** if step 3's green read each edition's copies without keeping

   bookshelf the reader does not belong to is left out*: `ROMANCE_DAWN` on

   alone. Mutation: the copies read through
   `copies.findByEditionIds(…)` of the series' editions, unfiltered.
6. **Guard.** *a series only on bookshelves the reader does not belong to
   is no such series*: Sam's own bookshelf holds `ROMANCE_DAWN`, Léa's

7. Tidy (`code-smells`), `./gradlew detekt`, the gate plainly, `tests=`,

## Risks and decisions
**Fit.** One run: one use case grown over existing ports, one new test
class of five cases, one un-skip. No migration, no contract change.

**The summary through `ListCatalogueSeries`.** The line asks for the name,
count, cover and kind the series list answers, by the same rules. Those
rules live in `ListCatalogueSeries`'s private `seriesOf` and in
`CatalogueEditions.findFirstOfEachSeries`; calling `listCatalogueSeries
(readerId)` and keeping the series of that id reuses them whole and
answers `NoSuchSeries` for any series outside the reader's catalogue, S5's
two cases included. A use case calling another is the tree's already
(`AddBookToBookshelf` calls `HoldEdition`). Its price is a few queries read
twice, the reader's bookshelves and copies; a household's catalogue makes
that cheap. Rejected: `seriesOf` made public or moved to a top-level
function (D10 forbids widening it); a factory on `CatalogueSeries` (D12
keeps the read's data class without rules); the rules written again in the
find (two copies of S2's and S3's rules); a query port for the series'
editions (the repositories serve this read within a few queries, D12).

**The books built from the reader's copies.** The reader's bookshelves,
their copies, those copies' editions kept when their `seriesVolume`
names the series, each with its copies grouped, as `BrowseCatalogue`
builds a page's `HeldEdition`s. Each book's `SeriesEntry` comes from
`seriesVolume.entryIn(series)` over the series `seriesRepository.findById`
answers, as the line says. Rejected: a `SeriesEntry` built by hand from
`CatalogueSeries.name` (it would bypass `entryIn`'s check and the line's
read); `copies.findByEditionIds` of every edition of the series (no port
lists a series' editions, and D12 gives a series no list of them).

**Steps 4 and 5 as red-or-guard.** Whether step 3's simplest green maps
copies or editions is the implementer's; T110's brief used the same form.
Either way the case stays, and the PR says which it was.
