## Goal
A tap on a row of the catalogue or of a series' page opens
`/catalogue/ouvrages/{id}`, which shows the lookup's card for that ouvrage,
its title the screen's `h1`, with the reader's bookshelves holding it. The
catalogue and the series' page then lead somewhere, and the page is ready
for T115's failure.

## Risks and decisions
**The page shows the lookup's card.** The spec says the card is the
lookup's and what it shows is its own tests', so `EditionPageView` draws
`SourceEditionCard` with `held`, and only its heading changes. Rejected: a
`HeldEditionCard` of its own (the card and its 40 cases copied).

**The card's `edition` is a `SourceEdition | Book`.** A `Book` holds every
field the card reads, its `isbn13` nullable, and the card already draws no
row for a `null` field. Rejected: `SourceEdition.isbn13` nullable (the
lookup's edition always has one); a mapping in the view (no ISBN to give).

**The overline leaves the heading on both screens; `pageTitle` chooses the
level.** One markup for one card: the lookup's text, order and spacing are
unchanged, its `h2` now named after the title alone, which no case of the
lookup reads. Rejected: the overline inside on the lookup and outside on
the page (two markups, one untested); a numeric level prop (two values
only, one of them the page's).

**The stored cover is one candidate, named `Libris`, shown `held`.** That
is how the lookup hands the card a held edition's stored cover (covers S10,
`ONE_PIECE_2_OWNED`); `held` draws neither dots nor a name, so the source
is never read. Rejected: a cover prop beside `covers` (a second way to
show a cover).

**The link lives in the row.** `CatalogueRow`'s root becomes a
`RouterLink` to `/catalogue/ouvrages/{id}`, as `SeriesRow`'s is, so both
screens share one address. Rejected: each view wrapping the row in a link
(the address written twice, the row's spec unable to prove it).

**Steps 6 and 7 cover the page's cover only.** The bookshelf rows, the
field rows and the summary are what the card draws from its props,
copies passed as they come: S1 proves the rows, the card's spec the rest.
The back button and the layout against the mockups have no test in the
sandbox: the PR leaves them to Tophe.
