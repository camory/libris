## Goal
A tap on a row of the series list opens `/catalogue/series/{id}`, which
shows the series' name, its count *dans vos bibliothèques.* and its
editions in the order a shelf reads. The page then has the header and the
list that T105 to T107 fill in, with no port or route change.

## Risks and decisions
**The row learns one prop.** The page's row is the catalogue's row with a
shorter overline, so `CatalogueRow` takes `withoutSeriesName`, false by
default, and picks `catalogue.volume.<kind>` with it, the series' name
being the page's title. The catalogue keeps its rows untouched. Rejected:
a second row component (the whole row twice); the overline's text passed
in by the view (the kind's words would leave the row for each view).

**The hint reuses the list's count.** `seriesPage.hint` is *{count} dans
vos bibliothèques.*, filled with the series list's `series.count.<kind>`
words, the spec saying the hint is series-list S2's count. Rejected: a
second set of count entries per kind (one word in two places).

**The order is the view's, through the domain.** `booksInOrder` beside
`Book`, called by the view in a `computed`, as `SeriesView` sorts the
series. A book without a series sorts as one without a tome; the answer
never holds one. Rejected: the composable sorting (a `locale` in
`application`).

**The series row is a link as a whole.** `SeriesRow` holds its
`RouterLink`, as `BookshelfRow` holds its own; it has no control, so
whether the link wraps the row or stretches over it is the implementer's.

**The home row's star keeps its place.** With the chevron gone, nothing
takes its space: the star's 44 box ends the row. Checked on the Pixel with
the spec's *Done*. Rejected: a margin in the chevron's place (no rule or
mockup asks one).

**S1 un-skipped last, a guard**, as in T103: by step 12 it passes.
