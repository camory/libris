## Risks and decisions
**Edition-page S1 drops the count from its expected row.** The spec says
S1, which reads the row's words, moves with the row, and that what the
card shows is the card's own tests'. Its `rows()` reads the element whose
text starts *Dans*; once the count is an element of its own, that is the
name's line. Rejected: a `getByText("2 exemplaires")` added to S1 (more
than a move: the count is the card's tests'); a `rows()` reading the whole
row (its text glues *Léa2 exemplaires*, `frontend.md`'s compiler item).

**Two catalogue entries, `isbn.card.bookshelf` and `isbn.card.count`,
the count drawn only past one copy.** Each line is a string of its own,
styled apart, and a row of one copy draws no second line, so the count
needs no plural form; the name line is a plain `t`, no `<i18n-t>`.
Rejected: keeping the two-form `isbn.card.copies` with slots (one sentence
cannot become two lines, and its first form would be empty); renaming
`catalogue.copies` (the catalogue keeps its rows).

**The sizes are checked by hand.** jsdom lays nothing out (`frontend.md`):
the 44, the 12 above the words and the icons on the first line are
Tophe's, against the mockups. With a count the row grows past 44 to hold
its second line; 44 is its least height, as `1-owned-and-guest.jpg` draws.

**S4's "sends nothing" holds by construction.** The card has no port and
emits nothing yet; S4's `removal.asked` stays empty whatever *Annuler*
does. T134, which gives *Retirer* its send, is where a test proves
*Annuler* sends nothing beside it. Rejected: an emitted-event assertion
now (no event exists to assert absent).

**Steps 4 to 6 are guards.** T132 keyed the question by bookshelf id
(PROGRESS, 2026-11-07), so the question already moves; step 4 states it
at the card's level and S5 at the screen's, each with its mutation.
