## Risks and decisions
**The corner is a named slot, `corner`, on the card and on the form.** U06
says the screen gives the card its button and the card does not know what
it does; the view fills the slot with the pencil or the cross and holds the
state they toggle. A case mounts the slot with a plain button.
- Rejected: a prop `editable` and an emit `edit` (the card would know the act).
- Rejected: the button drawn by the view over the card (the corner is the card's layout).

**The shown cover crosses by `shownSource`, read once at mount.** The card
and the form are two components, so `CoverChoice`'s tap does not survive
the switch; the view hands back the `coverSource` it already keeps. The
lookup clears it, so that a new answer shows its first cover. This extends
U06's *the one tapped, else the first not failed* with *else the one the
screen names*, and U07's icon list with `Pencil`: proposed in the PR body,
`docs/DESIGN.md` left as is.
- Rejected: both kept mounted, one hidden (S1 and S13 read no *Titre* label, which a hidden field keeps).
- Rejected: `v-model:coverSource` (renames the emit the card, the form, the view and their specs share).

**The form is remounted on each pencil**, so the cross needs no reset and
the next pencil shows the answer (S13); a `KeepAlive` is S13's guard.

**The card's pencil and dots stay enabled while its add runs**, the spec
saying only that the form accepts nothing. The add's answer replaces card
and form alike, and a failed add's message stays under the button across
the pencil and the cross. Noted in `agent/PROPOSED.md` for Tophe.
- Rejected: disabling them (behaviour no scenario or *Screen* line states).
