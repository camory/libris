## Risks and decisions
**The sentence has a key of its own, `series.error`.** Every screen's
message has its key in that screen's block (`home.bookshelves.error`,
`bookshelf.error`, `catalogue.error`), and the series view keeps its own
words under `series` (`series.empty`, `series.cover`). Rejected: reusing
`catalogue.error` (the series view would read the ouvrages' list's
message; the two lists' words could no longer part).

**`IconAlert`, not Lucide's `CircleAlert`.** U07 keeps the hand-drawn
icons until a follow-up replaces each one everywhere; every message of
the tree draws `IconAlert`. Rejected: `CircleAlert` here alone (two alert
icons on screen depending on the list that failed).

**`unavailable` carries nothing.** The list asks once per arrival and has
no page already shown to keep, unlike the catalogue's `unavailable`
holding its `books`; it is `useListReaderBookshelves`'s state. Rejected:
`{ status: "unavailable"; series: [] }` (a field nothing reads).

**The *lists nothing* case is a guard.** Once step 3 draws the message in
a branch of its own, nothing else is drawn for `unavailable`; the case is
kept so that a message added beside the empty state, rather than in its
place, fails a unit case and not only S6.

**No case for a refused answer.** `useBrowseCatalogue.spec.ts` has one
for a port rejecting with a plain `Error`; here every rejection takes one
`catch`, so that case would be green on arrival and guard nothing step 2
does not.
