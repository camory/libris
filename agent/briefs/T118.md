## Goal
The card, on the lookup and on the ouvrage's page, shows the summary one
paragraph per line, folded after six lines on an ellipsis with *Lire la
suite* under it, which unfolds it for good. A long BnF blurb then no
longer buries the card's foot, and the summary reads as its paragraphs.

## Test plan
Gate: `cd frontend && npm test`; fast loop `npx vitest run <path>` from
`frontend/`. One step, one cycle, one commit.

The seam: jsdom lays nothing out and answers 0 to every layout measure, so
no summary overflows in any spec unless one says so. A case of an
overflowing summary stubs `scrollHeight` and `clientHeight` on
`HTMLElement.prototype` with `Object.defineProperty(…, { configurable:
true, get: () => n })` before it mounts, `scrollHeight` above
`clientHeight`; the file's `afterEach` removes both with
`Reflect.deleteProperty`, which gives jsdom's back (they live on
`Element.prototype`). This is why every existing spec, the scenarios
included, keeps seeing no button.

The three lines of the card's cases are the first three of
`SummaryScenarios.spec.ts`' *Mont Tombe*, or any three of the
implementer's; the paragraphs are read as the scenario's
`summaryParagraphs` reads them: `getAllByRole("paragraph")`, text trimmed,
kept when it is one of the lines.

## Risks and decisions
**The fold is drawn by the browser, the button decided by a measure.**
"Six lines" is six lines at the card's width in the reader's font, which
only the layout knows: the block is clamped at six lines with the
ellipsis (Tailwind's `line-clamp-6`), and the card offers *Lire la suite*
when the clamped block's `scrollHeight` exceeds its `clientHeight` once
mounted. Rejected: counting characters or lines of text (wrong on every
width); a `ResizeObserver` (jsdom has none, and nothing here resizes);
always offering the button (the spec's Short state has none).

**What the specs cannot see.** The clamp is a style: no spec reads it
(D07), so a tap that removes the button but leaves the clamp would pass
step 4. The spec places that proof on the Pixel, with the fold at six
lines and its absence on a short summary. The PR says so.

**The clamp over several paragraphs.** Chrome, the household's only
browser (D06), clamps through the block's child paragraphs and puts the
ellipsis on the sixth line overall. Under the clamp's `display:
-webkit-box` a `gap` may draw nothing: the 8 between two paragraphs is
safest as their margins. Both are checked on the Pixel against
`1-folded.jpg`; the brief does not fix the classes.

**The split stays in the component.** `summary.split("\n")` is one call
with no rule of the domain's; the spec proves it in the card's component
test. Rejected: a domain function (a test of `String.split`).

**The word's key is `isbn.card.readMore`**, beside the card's other words
under `isbn.card`, which the page's card reads too. Rejected: a key under
`editionPage` (the card is one component on both screens).

**S2 un-skipped last, a guard**, as in T115: by step 1 both cases pass.
