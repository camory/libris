the ouvrage's page. The card stays and its row shows one copy fewer, or
*Dans aucune de vos bibliothèques* with the button *Ajouter à ma
bibliothèque* under the card. This is the last task of `remove-copy`;
after it, Tophe checks the spec's *Done* on the Pixel.

## Risks and decisions
**The card drops the copy itself; Libris is not asked again.** The view
holds every copy it shows: the lookup's copies and the added one. After
a removal, or the not-found answer, it shows them without the removed
copy. Asking again would also contradict S8's case 1, whose fake answers
the two copies on every lookup. Rejected: a new lookup after the removal
(the skeleton flashes in place of the card the spec keeps, and the
request is one whose answer the view already holds).

**Any removal ends the *Added* state.** The spec says "the button gone
after an add (bookshelf S2) comes back once a copy is removed". Once the
reader acts on the card again, it is no longer the add's confirmation:
it is the card of an ISBN the house holds, in the *Already there* or
*Found* state, both with the button. Rejected: bringing the button back
only when the added copy goes (a narrower reading of "once a copy is
removed").

**After an add, the card left by a removal is the card of the house's
edition.** It shows the edition that was sent with the cover that was
sent, as held: no pencil and no dots. A new add sends that edition. The
add created the edition and the removal keeps it, so the ISBN is now one
the house holds, which `7-lookup-card-emptied.jpg` draws and the spec's
*Done* reads ("its card as the house holds it"). A failed new add then
leaves this card as bookshelf S5 says. Rejected, three alternatives:
- The lookup's answer shown again: edits made in the form are lost, and
  the pencil offers to edit an edition the house already holds.
- The added card kept with the button beside it: two paths for one card,
  and a failed new add would fall back to the lookup's answer.
- Mutating `useAddBookToBookshelf`'s state from the view.

How the view represents this state is the implementer's choice.

**A lookup forgets a removal still being sent**, as it already forgets
the add (`addBookToBookshelf` rebuilt on each search): the new card's
trash works and a late answer changes nothing on it (U05). How the view
does this is the implementer's choice. Rejected: disabling *Chercher*
while a removal is sent (the field and its button belong to the lookup,
and U04 keeps the field usable).
