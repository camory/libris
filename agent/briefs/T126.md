## Goal
While the reader types in *Série*, up to five series of the house that
contain the text show under the box, sorted, the matched part in
semibold; a tap puts one in the field; the names failing, the field works
as before. Every volume lands on the same series without retyping it.

## Risks and decisions
**S7's *a tap* moves to T127.** Its last line awaits *Existante*, T127's
word, so T126 cannot run it green as written, and the implementer would
block on it. The brief's commit amends the backlog: T126 un-skips S7 *the
suggestions* and S8, T127 also *a tap*, as the spec's *Tasks* section
already divides S7 (T126 the suggestions, T127 the word); the tap itself
is proven here by steps 13 and 14.
- Rejected: *Existante* in T126 (half of T127's word); `blocked` (the
  backlog is the planner's).

**The rule answers the parts.** `suggestionsFor` cuts each name in
`before`, `matched`, `after`: the domain proves the matched part, the
form draws three spans, the middle one `font-semibold`, checked by hand.
- Rejected: the form finding the match (D05); asserting a tag (D07).

**Contains, folded character by character.** A character of the name
and of the text is compared without its marks and in lower case in the
reader's locale, and the parts are cut on the name's own characters, so
an accent stored as a mark of its own stays in its part (step 7). A
folding that differs from `nameOrder`'s (*Ø* stays apart from *O*) is
accepted.
- Rejected: `Intl.Collator` (it compares, it cannot find a text inside).

**A blank text holds no text** ("ignoring the spaces at its ends"):
spaces alone suggest nothing, not five rows nobody asked for.

**The view asks for the names for each form.** `IsbnView` calls
`useListSeriesNames`' `list` for the form of each lookup and passes
`names` as `seriesNames`, `[]` until answered and when Libris does not
answer; when it asks within that is the implementer's.
- Rejected: once at mount (the series the last add created missing from
  the next form); the form calling the port (D05); a failed state (T127's).

**The press keeps the focus.** A row's `mousedown` is prevented: the
field keeps the focus and the keyboard, no blur closes the rows before
the click. Rejected: closing on blur after a delay (a timer racing it).

**The rows live in `EditionForm`**, one user; T127's language, the
second, extracts them. Rejected: a component for one caller.

**The scroll is the box's `scrollIntoView({ block: "start" })` on its
focus**, with optional chaining (jsdom has none); a form too short under
*Série* to reach the top is the hand check's to report, no padding added.

**The rows' text joins with no space.** Vue keeps a space between an
interpolation and an element on the next line: the three parts are
three elements, each on its own line, or the scenario's `suggestions`
reads *One  piece*.
