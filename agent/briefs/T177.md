## Goal
An expired session met on the lookup screen takes the reader through
Authelia as `/me` does, and a lookup whose request never comes back gives
*Chercher* back with no skeleton left standing. A reader on a dropping
network or a stale session can then try again instead of facing a screen
frozen for ever.

## Risks and decisions
**The `401` rejects, as `/me`'s does.** The client calls the callback,
then rejects with an `Error`; the view, reading the rejection as no
answer, gives the button back while the navigation leaves the page.
Rejected: answering a problem outcome for the `401` (a status the contract
does not declare would read as an answer of Libris); a callback that
returns a never-settling promise (the screen stays busy if the navigation
is refused).

**The catch lives in the view's `search()`.** `IsbnView` calls the port
itself today, and the line is a bug, not the lookup moved into a
composable; catching there is what T175 did inside `useSearchCatalogue`,
one level down. Rejected: catching in `FetchIsbnApi` (the `401` must
reject, and a client hiding a network failure as a problem would lie
about Libris); a `useLookUpIsbn` composable (a refactor beyond the bug,
for `agent/PROPOSED.md` if it bites).

**Nothing is said after a rejection.** The line forbids a sentence and the
PRD keeps what to say open, so the space under the field is empty, as
before the first search. Rejected: showing `isbn.error` (*Erreur lors de
la recherche…*), which decides the open question.

**`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` lags.** D06 says *The frontend does not handle
the 401 yet; the task that adds it…* and D07 *the 401 of D06 is outside
the contract and waits for its own task*; both were already untrue since
T012 for `/me`. A run may not edit them silently: the PR body proposes
that D06 say `bootstrap` hands one `onUnauthenticated` to `FetchMeApi`
and `FetchIsbnApi`, and that D07 say a client's `401` case runs over a
stubbed `fetch`.
