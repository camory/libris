## Goal
*Enregistrer* on the ouvrage's page sends the form through `editionsApi`,
busy while it runs, and once saved the card shows the book Libris
answered, the form gone. T163 then gives the refused or failed save its
message.

## Risks and decisions
**The composable takes the form's edition and drops its ISBN.** The form
emits a `SourceEdition`, `isbn13` included, and the port takes
`EditionDetails`; TypeScript lets the larger object through, so the drop
must be written and proven where the save is decided, the composable's
step 2. Trap: `const { isbn13, ...details }` reds ESLint, whose
`no-unused-vars` has no `ignoreRestSiblings`. Rejected: the view dropping
it (a rule in the view, proven only by the scenario); the client dropping
it (the fake records what the port is given, so S4 reds).

**The saved book goes to the page's find through a new `show(book)`.**
The card, the copies a removal counts and the next pencil all read
`useFindCatalogueEdition`'s state, so the answer of the save must land
there; `show` sets it with no request. The view acts on the save's
outcome after awaiting it, as `BookshelfView` does after `addMember`.
Rejected: `refresh(id)` after the save (a second request, and S4's fake
catalogue answers the old book); the view writing the composable's `state`
ref from outside; a saved book kept beside the found one (a later
removal's `refresh` would be hidden under it).

**The state names follow `AddState`.** `ready`, `saving`, `saved` with
the book; T163 adds the outcome of a refusal with its cases. Until T163, a
problem answer leaves the state `saving`: no case of this task reaches it,
and the next task in the backlog replaces it.

**The busy word is the dock's, chosen by `action`.** `DockedAdd` already
picks its label from `action`; its busy word follows the same prop.
Rejected: a busy label passed as text (the component picks its words from
keys, U08).

**The cross is disabled, not hidden, while the save runs.** U04: the form,
its cross included, accepts nothing and looks the same.
