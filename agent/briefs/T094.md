## Goal
`ListCatalogueSeries` answers each series with the kind every one of its
editions shares, or `null` when two of them differ, where T093 left `kind`
`null`. With T093's count, S2 is whole on the backend; the frontend words a
count in albums or tomes from this kind (T097).

## Rules in play
- D02 — the change stays inside `ListCatalogueSeries`; the controller,

  `ApiContractTest` stay as T093 left them.
- D07 — the shared kind is tested where it is computed, in

## Test plan
Suite: `cd backend && ./gradlew check`. Each step one case, one cycle, one
commit, in this order; `./gradlew detekt` before each commit. Each green is
the smallest that passes the cases so far, since the next step's red
depends on it; all three cases sit in `ListCatalogueSeriesTest` and assert
the whole answer, `series shouldBe listOf(CatalogueSeries(...))`, so each
proves the count and the kind of its series together.

1. **Red, `ListCatalogueSeriesTest`** (`ListCatalogueSeries`). The
   existing case `a series counts its editions, each once however many
   copies it has` expects `kind` `MANGA`, the kind its two manga editions
   share; nothing else in it changes, its title included. Red: the use
   case answers `null`. Green, the smallest: every series answers `MANGA`.
2. **Red, `ListCatalogueSeriesTest`** (`ListCatalogueSeries`).
   `a series whose editions are all BD has the kind BD`: Léa owns a
   bookshelf holding one copy each of *Astérix le Gaulois* (`Astérix`,
   tome 1, `BD`) and *La serpe d'or* (`Astérix`, tome 2, `BD`); answered
   `listOf(CatalogueSeries("Astérix", 2, null, BD))`. Red: step 1 answers
   `MANGA`. Green, the smallest: a series answers the kind of one of its
   editions, so the grouping keeps each series' editions, not only its
   name.
3. **Red, `ListCatalogueSeriesTest`** (`ListCatalogueSeries`).
   `a series mixing a BD and a manga has no kind`: Léa owns a bookshelf
   holding one copy each of two editions of `Lastman`, tome 1 a `BD` and
   tome 2 a `MANGA`; answered
   `listOf(CatalogueSeries("Lastman", 2, null, null))`. Red: step 2 answers
   the kind of one of its editions, whichever it is. Green: the kind every
   edition of the series shares, `null` when two differ.
4. Tidy, `./gradlew detekt`, gate plainly, tick T094, PROGRESS entry, PR.

Not to be written: a `BOOK` case (the rule names no kind, and the three
cases of the spec are these); a case of three editions where two agree
(the rule is "every edition", which step 3 already reds); a case on the
order of the answer or of a series' editions; any case on `coverName`
(T095); a domain, JDBC, web or scenario test.

## Risks and decisions
**Fit.** One run: one use case changed, three unit cases, two code files.
Rejected: a split (each case is a few lines of fixture over the same read).

**The shared kind is the use case's, beside the count.** No aggregate
holds a series' editions: a series is a value of the edition (D12), so the
kind they share is the read's own work, as T093 made the count, and the
use case that groups the editions is where both rules are seen and tested.
D12's "a collection with a rule of its own is a type of its own" speaks of
a collection an aggregate holds; none holds this one. Rejected:
`Kind.sharedBy(kinds)` on the enum's companion (a second test class for one
expression, and the count would stay in the use case beside it); a domain
type for a series' editions now (whether T095's pick by position, with an
order, needs one is T095's brief's call; if it does, the kind moves with
the count into it then).

**The existing case is the manga series.** T093's case already holds two
manga editions of `One piece`, one in two copies; its expected `kind` was
`null` only until this task. Changing that one value makes it the manga
case of the backlog line and S2's first red, and keeps its title, which
names the count it still proves. Rejected: a fourth case for the manga
series (it would repeat this one's editions); renaming the case (its title
is the count's rule, unchanged).

**Greens kept the smallest.** Step 1's green is the constant `MANGA` and
step 2's the kind of one edition, so that steps 2 and 3 are each red before
green and no step is a guard. Rejected: the shared kind written at step 1
(steps 2 and 3 would pass on arrival, guards whose mutations prove less
than a red).

**Fixtures.** *Astérix le Gaulois* and *La serpe d'or* are the first two
albums of `Astérix`, both BD; `Lastman` is a French series printed in a
manga's format, which a household may well record as a BD for one tome
and a manga for another: the mixed case as it happens. Each case's
editions have one copy each on Léa's own bookshelf: the copies counted
once are T093's case's to show.
