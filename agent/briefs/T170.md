## Goal
The box asks Libris once the reader pauses 300 ms, or at once on the
keyboard's search key, which closes the keyboard; a text of fewer than two
characters asks nothing and gives back the whole catalogue. Without it, a
word typed at speed sends one request per keystroke.

## Risks and decisions
**The pause lives in `SearchField`.** The field holds the keystrokes and
the keyboard's key, so it decides when the text is asked and says so by
`search`; the view passes it to `useSearchCatalogue`, whose `search(text)`
asks at once. T171's cross emits it at once, T176 reads it. The field
calls the global `setTimeout` at each keystroke, never a reference taken
earlier, or S6's fake timers, installed after the mount, miss it.
Rejected: the pause in the composable (a second verb, or a flag, beside
`search`, D10); in the view (a timer and a key handler for the field's
own events).

**The search key is the form's submit.** The box sits in a `<form>` whose
submit is prevented: a browser maps the keyboard's key to the form's
implicit submission; the field emits at once, drops the pending pause and
blurs the box, which closes the keyboard. Rejected: a `keydown` listener
on `Enter` (an IME's key does not always arrive as one).

**A short text waits for the pause too.** Every keystroke restarts the
pause; at its end, a text under two characters gives back the whole
catalogue. The spec makes "at once" the cross's alone. Rejected: the
catalogue at once on a short text (the rows would jump while the reader
retypes over a search).

**`searchQueryOf` answers `null` under two characters**, the trimmed text
otherwise, as `languageCode` answers `null` for what it refuses; the
composable maps `null` to `ready`, asking nothing. Rejected: the length
read in the field or the composable (U04 puts the rule reading a field in
the domain).

**No timer is cleared on unmount.** A pause running when the reader leaves
the catalogue ends on an unmounted field, whose `emit` Vue 3.5 drops
(`if (instance.isUnmounted) return`); no case can red on it.

**The rows kept while answered need no code.** `useSearchCatalogue` has no
waiting state, so the rows shown stay until the answer replaces them;
step 5 guards it.
