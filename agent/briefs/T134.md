## Risks and decisions
**The page asks the ouvrage again through `find`, its skeleton
included.** `find` shows the card's skeleton then the new answer, and the
card mounted afresh draws no question, so nothing resets the row's
`asking` by hand. The flash is the page's own loading state; Tophe judges
it on the Pixel. Rejected: keeping the card while asking again (a second
way to find in the composable, and the question to reset by hand).

**The card hands up a copy id, chosen from the row's bookshelf.** The
rows group the copies by bookshelf; which copy goes is not promised (the
spec), so any copy of the asking row will do, the order of step 7 only
refusing another bookshelf's. Rejected: emitting the bookshelf's id (the
view would have to find a copy again); a copy id in `Holding` (the domain
grows for one component's choice).

**`removing` is a prop, the state the composable's.** As `adding` and
`creating` reach `DockedAdd` and `NewBookshelfRow`: the view owns the
removal and the card draws it. Optional, so `EditionForm` and `IsbnView`
pass nothing. Rejected: the card holding the removal (a port in
`ui/components`).

**The busy check's label is *Retrait en cours…*.** Every busy control of
the tree names the act in progress (*Création en cours…*, *Ajout en
cours…*, *Changement en cours…*), and *retrait* is the word of the
spec's message. Rejected: keeping *Retirer* on a disabled button (a
label for an act the button no longer offers).

**Twelve scenario files gain one line**, as when `catalogueApi` (T046),
`readersApi` (T083) and `seriesApi` (T096) joined: `vue-tsc` refuses a
ports object without the port. `FakeCopiesApi` rejects only when asked.

**The copy id is not encoded in the path**: a uuid Libris answered, never
read from the address.
