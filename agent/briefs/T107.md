## Goal
The series page opens with the series' cover as a band above its name, so
the reader recognises the series at a glance, as on the shelf. A series
with no cover, or whose cover does not load, opens on its name as before.

## Risks and decisions
**The band lives in `SeriesPageView`, no component.** The header's top
padding depends on whether the band shows, so whoever knows the band
failed must also lay out the header: the view holds it, keyed to the
series shown (step 4). Rejected: a `SeriesBand` component emitting its
failure to the view (an event and a prop pair for one image); a generic
`PageBand` (one screen has a band; `CLAUDE.md`: no speculative abstraction).

**The name is `series.cover`, *Couverture de {name}*.** The series list
already names the same picture of the same series with it; one key keeps
both screens' words one. Rejected: a `seriesPage.cover` holding the same
words twice.

**Full width across the content's padding, checked by hand.** `<main>`
carries the content's `px-5` and the column's `max-w-120`; the band
spans the column, padding included, as the mockup draws it edge to edge.
How it crosses the padding (negative side margins on the band, or the
padding moved to the blocks under it) is the implementer's, said in the PR
body: it is the convention the next screen with a band will follow, hence
*First of its kind*. jsdom lays nothing out, so the crop
(`object-cover` from the top), the 130, the width and the header's 12
are left to Tophe on the Pixel, as the task line says. Rejected: a test
on classes (D07 forbids it).

**The card's space needs no change.** The header ends with its 12 and the
card, first of the `gap-5` column, follows with no margin of its own: the
"no other space" of the task line already holds, band or none.

**S4 un-skipped last, a guard**, as in T103 to T106: by step 4 it passes.
Its *a cover that does not load* fires `error` through
`@testing-library/dom`'s `fireEvent`, which reaches the same handler as
step 3.
