## Goal
The frontend reads contract `v0.16.0`: every copy it receives names its
bookshelf with the reader's role, and the card draws the trash on a row
the reader owns, which asks its question when tapped. T133 to T137 make
the question act, on the ouvrage's page then on the lookup screen.

## Risks and decisions
**The deploy precondition is Tophe's.** As T087, T103 and T121: no run
can see what is deployed; the brief is written on T131 deployed, since
Tophe launches the loop. The PR body says merging is not deploying.

**`ReaderBookshelf` in `src/domain/Copy.ts`, `Bookshelf` with an optional
`role: MembershipRole`.** Scenario files of several specs, `RemoveCopyScenarios`
included, build copies from a `Bookshelf` (`on(bookshelf: Bookshelf):
Copy`, `{ id, bookshelf: salon }`), and D07 forbids changing them for
this; #340 put the role in S3's data for that reason. Optional, they
compile as they are, and a copy without a role is read as one the reader
does not own: no trash. The response types of `infra/api` declare it
required, as the contract does. The name is the contract's. Rejected: a
required role (vue-tsc reds the scenario files, a spec conversation);
`role` on `Bookshelf` (every bookshelf screen and `Membership` would carry
a role beside their own).

**The question is the card's own state, in `BookshelvesHolding`.** The
rows are drawn there and the card is shown by two views; no port is
involved yet. T134 gives it what it sends. Rejected: the state in each
view (two copies of it, a third with the lookup).

**S5 may already pass.** A question kept for one bookshelf at a time
moves on another trash's tap; S5 stays skipped, as the line says, and
T133 un-skips it.

**The trash shows on every card, the lookup's too**, as U06 and the
spec's *Screen* draw it; it asks there and does nothing more until T137.
Rejected: a prop hiding it per screen (a choice the spec does not make).

**Words.** One sub-block `isbn.card.removal` beside `isbn.card.copies`,
the row's words, which T135 extends with its message. The non-breaking
space is written `\u00A0` in `i18n.ts`, through `sed` as
`every-run.md` says, since a typed escape arrives as the literal; the
scenario's normalised query still finds it.

**Colours on the icons.** `IconButton` carries `text-accent`; a second
text colour in its `class` is settled by Tailwind's emit order, so the
cross's `muted` and the check's `danger` go on the icon, whose own colour
beats the one it inherits (`frontend.md`, two utilities for one property).
