## Risks and decisions
**The copies sit beside the edition, not in it.** The found answer becomes
`{outcome, edition, copies}`; `SourceEdition` is unchanged. The edition is what
the sources and the house know of an ISBN, the copies are the reader's; T039
posts the edition "its copies apart", and the spec keeps `NewBook` apart from
the lookup answer. The client builds the edition field by field so the copies
cannot ride along in it.
- Rejected: `SourceEdition.copies` — T039 would strip it before posting, and
  every edition fixture would carry a field of the library.
- Rejected: a new port for the copies — the contract answers them in the lookup.

**The rows are grouped by bookshelf id, named by the answer, in the order of
first appearance.** Two bookshelves may share a name; the id is what makes them
one. The card sorts nothing, as it sorts no author. The grouping stays in the
component, as `authorLines` groups the authors.
- Rejected: a domain function grouping copies — one caller, and the precedent
  groups in the component.
- Rejected: sorting by name — U06 has the card sort nothing.

**One catalogue entry with two forms, `isbn.card.copies`.** The one-copy form
reads *Dans {bookshelf}*, the other *Dans {bookshelf} · {count} exemplaires*,
and the component hands the count and the name. The component holds no
comparison with one and no French; a count of zero never reaches it, since a
bookshelf with no copy has no row. The count is in `muted`, the name in `text`,
as the author line puts its roles after the dot (U06); the row is in `body`.
- Rejected: two entries and a `v-if` on the count — a branch in the component.
- Rejected: *Dans* and *exemplaires* as two entries joined in the template —
  the sentence split over two keys.

**The rows are a part of their own, between the top part and the field rows.**
The spec places them between the authors and the rows and U06 knows neither
place nor look for them; the PR body proposes to `docs/DESIGN.md` a sentence
for U06: "Between the top part and the rows, one row per bookshelf holding a
copy, `body`, *Dans* and the name in `text`, the count after a middle dot in
`muted`, in the order of the answer." Tophe settles it in review.
- Rejected: the rows inside the right column under the authors — a long
  bookshelf name would wrap beside the cover.

**Both S4 scenario tests are un-skipped.** The line cites *S4 The ouvrage is
already in a bookshelf*, which is the prefix of both titles, and says the task
realises S4 on the frontend; the second test is S4's own case, the card
*without a place*, and no later task cites it. The PR body says so.
- Rejected: leaving *none of the reader's* skipped — orphaned, the phase's
  *Done* asks Tophe for that card.

**The copies of the view's and card's specs are written in each spec.** Only
two specs use them, each with its own variants; `barelyKnown` and `asterix1`
set the precedent of local fixtures.
- Rejected: `src/fixture/Copies.ts` — a shared file for two literals.

**The spec says `v0.6.0`, the line says `v0.6.2`.** `v0.6.1` changed the uuid
pattern and `v0.6.2` added the blank refusals of `NewBook` (PROGRESS, T037);
the backend pins `v0.6.2`. The line is followed; `specs/bookshelf.md` is not
edited by a run.
