# T086 — The card and a catalogue row sort their authors and bookshelves

**Backlog line:**
> - [ ] T086 Frontend: the card and a catalogue row sort their authors and
>       bookshelves.
>       The card sorts its authors by their first role, scénario, dessin,
>       couleurs, traduction, then by name, and its rows *Dans …* by name; a
>       catalogue row sorts its authors in the card's order and its
>       bookshelves by name; each name ignoring case and accents, compared in
>       the reader's language, the comparison the bookshelves' screens sort
>       with (U06, D11). Unit tests of both orders, over names
>       that differ only in case or accents, given in another order than the
>       screen's.

`specs/catalogue.md` *Screen* (a row's authors in the card's order, its

**First of its kind:** the first order the frontend computes, and

## Goal
The card and a catalogue row order their authors and their bookshelves
themselves, through pure functions of `domain/` over one comparison of
names in the screen's language, so that they read as today once the
backend answers in no promised order (T087).

## Rules in play
- D11 — a complete list shown to a reader is sorted by the screen, with
  `Intl.Collator` in the reader's language; nothing relies on the order of
  `authors` or `copies` in an answer. The catalogue's rows keep the order of
  the answer: a page's order is the backend's.
- D05 — the orders are pure functions of `src/domain`, imported by the
  components; no port, composable or dependency; the locale comes from
  `useI18n().locale` in the component, never from `navigator`.
- D07 — `domain` specs are plain Vitest, no doubles; component specs mount
  with props and `createLibrisI18n()` and assert the text and its order,
  never a tag or class; no scenario, fixture or `infra/api` spec changes;
  each guard's mutation run, reverted, listed in the PR body; gate
  `cd frontend && npm test`, run plainly.
- D10 — names in PRD §3's words: `author`, `bookshelf`, `copy`, never
  `shelf` or `credit`; a file reads top-down; scope `frontend`, Conventional
  Commits, trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- U06 — the card's authors by their first role, in the order WRITER,
  ARTIST, COLOURIST, TRANSLATOR (scénario, dessin, couleurs, traduction),
  then by name; an author's roles on their line in that same order; the
  grouping by whole set of roles unchanged; the rows *Dans …* by bookshelf
  name; every name ignoring case and accents.
- `specs/catalogue.md` *Screen* — a row's authors, names alone, in the
  card's order, each once; its bookshelves by name, each with its count.
- `CLAUDE.md` — TDD, no rationale nor `D##`/`U##` in code; the role order
  written out in the domain, not read from `AuthorRole`'s declaration.

## Risks and decisions
**Fit.** One run: three small domain functions with their specs, two
components rewired; about ten files, no dependency, no contract. Rejected: a
split card / row (the row's order is the card's: one function serves both).

**The orders are domain functions, taking the locale.** D05 places sort
orders in `domain`, and the row must follow the card's author order, which
only a shared function guarantees. The grouping moves with them, since the
first role needs an author's roles together and the count a bookshelf's
copies. Rejected: sorting in each component (the author order twice, and
the orders proven only by mounting); an `application` composable (no port,
no state).

**The locale is the screen's, given by the component.** The reader's
language is the one the screen speaks, `createLibrisI18n`'s `fr`;
`useI18n().locale.value` hands it to the domain, so the language is stated
once. Rejected: `"fr"` written in `domain` (a second statement of the
locale); `Intl.Collator()` with no locale, or `navigator.language` (the
browser's, not the screen's; `en-US` in the sandbox; `main.ts` alone reads
`window`).

**`sensitivity: "base"`.** *Ignoring case and accents* means names apart by
case or accents only compare equal; the stable sort then keeps the answer's
order between them, as T074 left it on the backend. Rejected: the
collator's default sensitivity (it orders by accent, then case, once the
letters tie: not ignoring them).

**An author's roles read in role order on their line.** *First role*
presupposes that order, and the line reads it today only because the
backend sorts, which T087 stops; the phase sorts what the screens show
today. Rejected: the answer's order (the line would change with T087).

**Grouping by exact name, as today.** The backend matches author names
(PRD §3); the card's `Map` by name moves into `authorsInOrder` unchanged.
Rejected: grouping names equal under `nameOrder` (no document says so).

**The examples *Erckmann, Chatrian* of U06 and `specs/kind.md` contradict
U06's rule, *then by name*.** The rule is explicit and newer (#234); the
example only shows the commas. The card's case follows the rule, and the PR
body proposes *Chatrian, Erckmann* for both documents, which a run may not
edit. Rejected: `blocked` (the rule leaves no doubt).
