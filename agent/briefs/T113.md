## Goal
`/catalogue/ouvrages/{id}` exists: the card's skeleton while the ouvrage
comes, then *Ouvrage introuvable.* when Libris answers no such ouvrage or
the id is not one, over contract `v0.14.0`. The catalogue's port then
holds the find the page needs, so T114 and T115 fill the page with no
port change.

## Risks and decisions
**The deploy precondition is Tophe's.** As T087, T096 and T103: no run
can see what is deployed; the brief is written on T112 deployed, since
Tophe launches the loop. The PR body says merging is not deploying.

**The composable knows loading and not found alone.** `found` comes red
with T114, `unavailable` with T115; until T114 any answer reads as no such
ouvrage, on an address no link reaches. Rejected: `found` now (no test
asks for it).

**The fake gains a second parameter.** Every `new FakeCatalogueApi(pages)`
stays as it is; the find defaults to a rejection, as `FakeSeriesApi`'s does.
S1's `edition` and `catalogue` fakes become one, since `LibrisPorts` has
one slot. Rejected: an answers object (every caller rewritten for one
method).

**Names.** The view `EditionPageView` in `ui/views/editionPage`, its words
in an `editionPage` block of the catalogue, after the spec and as
`seriesPage`; the composable `useFindCatalogueEdition`, after the
backend's use case, as `useFindCatalogueSeries`; the answer keeps #301's
`FindBookAnswer`. The route segment `ouvrages` is the spec's.

**A skeleton of its own.** `HeldEditionCardSkeleton` is the card as it
shows an edition the house holds: `SourceEditionCardSkeleton`'s top part,
then one bookshelf row (a 22 block and a bar), then three field rows (a
hairline above, a label bar at the left, a value bar at the right), within
the card's outline and padding. The lookup's skeleton keeps its top part
alone. Whether the two share their top part is the refactor step's
choice. Its shape is not asserted by any test (`aria-hidden` blocks): the
PR leaves the comparison with `1-loading.jpg` to Tophe. Rejected: props on
`SourceEditionCardSkeleton` (a branch no test motivates); a bare
`FieldRowSkeleton` ×3 (the series card's row, 14 of padding, not a card
field row).

**S2 un-skipped last, a guard**, as in T103: by step 13 it passes.
