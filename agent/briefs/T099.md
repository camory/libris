## Risks and decisions
**One component, `CatalogueSwitch`, drawn by each view.** Both views
already draw the catalogue's header themselves; the switch goes under it
in each, the same component twice. Rejected: a layout view on the
`/catalogue` parent drawing the header, the switch and a nested
`RouterView` (moves both headers, turns a component-less parent into a
view, a structure no task has asked for). Rejected: the switch in
`App.vue` (the shell owns the tab bar and the banner, never a screen's
block).

**Specific, not generic.** U04's segmented choice has one use; the
component knows its two links and its words. Rejected: a
`SegmentedChoice` taking `{ name, to }` halves (an abstraction with one
caller).

**Plain `RouterLink`s, current through `exact-active-class`.** Measured
while planning over `createLibrisRouter()`: on `/catalogue` the *Ouvrages*
link carries `aria-current="page"` and `router-link-exact-active`, *Séries*
neither; on `/catalogue/series` *Séries* carries both, *Ouvrages* only
`router-link-active`. So the current half's `bg-accent text-white` go in
`exact-active-class` and `text-muted` on the `nav`, the ancestor, as the
tab bar does, so the state wins whatever Tailwind's order. Rejected: the
tab's `custom` slot over `isActive` (marks *Ouvrages* on the series too,
step 5's mutation).

**The words under `catalogue.switch`.** The switch is the catalogue's,
drawn on both of its views, like `catalogue.title` and `catalogue.hint`
the series view already reads. `books` for *Ouvrages*, as `Book` names an
ouvrage of the catalogue in code. Rejected: `tabs` (the tab bar's words),
`series` (the series view's own words, while the switch is on both).

**Steps 4 and 5 are guards.** Step 3's `RouterLink`s already write the
mark exactly on the view shown; the two cases are kept so that a half
rewritten by hand, or from `isActive`, fails a unit case and not only S4.

**Two existing view specs change.** Both `open()`s gain the router, since
a `RouterLink` without one renders no link; *offers nothing to follow on a
row* asserted no link in the whole view, which the switch now holds, and
is scoped to the list it means. No other case changes.
