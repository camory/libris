## Goal
A tap on an author's roles' line opens, under the box, the four roles as
toggles, and what the reader chooses there is what the add sends. The
list closes on its line or on a tap anywhere else, and the line accepts
nothing while the add runs.

## Risks and decisions
**The card's order is the domain's, through two functions.**
`SourceEdition.ts` already holds it, private, for `authorsInOrder`; the
list needs the four roles in that order and a toggle that keeps the roles
in it, so the line reads them in the card's order as the *Screen* says.
`roleChoicesOf(roles): { role: AuthorRole; chosen: boolean }[]` and
`rolesToggled(roles, role): AuthorRole[]` are the module's new doors,
named as its neighbours (`authorsInOrder`, `suggestionsFor`). Rejected:
exporting `ROLES` (a private member made public, D10); the four roles
written again in `AuthorBox` (the order in two places); the toggle in the
component (a rule outside the domain).

**The box holds the list open; the form holds the roles.** The open state
is the box's alone, a line of its own; the roles are the form's data, so
`roles` becomes a model the form binds both ways, as `name` is. Rejected:
an event `toggle` with the role (the form would compute the roles); the
form holding which list is open (one more piece of state for what the
box decides).

**"Anywhere else" is a `click` on `document`.** A tap on the page, on
another field, on the box's own name or on the add closes the list; a tap
on its line toggles it and a tap on a row keeps it. Steps 10 and 11 fix
the event a test dispatches, `click`; the listener is removed when the
box unmounts. Rejected: `focusout` (a tap on a blank part of the page
moves no focus a detached spec can see, and the roles' line must not be
the only way in); the box's root as the listener's target (step 11).

**The toggles are not disabled while the add runs.** The add's tap is a
tap outside every box, so no list is open while the add runs, and the
lines, `disabled`, reopen none: the toggles cannot be reached. A
`disabled` on them would have no case that reds. Rejected: disabling them
anyway (code no test motivates).

**No case rewords the open list on a kind change.** A tap on a kind is a
tap outside the box, which closes the list; steps 5 and 14 cover the rest.
