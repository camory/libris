## Risks and decisions
**`EditionForm` takes the answer whole.** Props `isbn13`, `edition?:
SourceEdition` (absent for an unknown ISBN), `copies` and `covers`,
required, and the emit `coverSource`, as the card's. The fields take their
text once, at setup: numbers as their digits, `null` as `""`, the language
through `languageWord`, the kind from `edition.kind`; a new lookup remounts
the form through the skeleton, as T121's guard holds. Rejected: an
`initial` record of strings built by the view (the view would format).

**After the add, the form stays**, the button gone, its row counting the
added copy, as the card did: covers S3 reads the tapped cover and its
source after the add, which a remounted card would reset. The spec's
*Added* state, the card of the fields sent, is T123's with the edited add.
Rejected: swapping to the card now (covers S3 red until T123).

**The authors are worded by the answer's kind**; T125 words them by the
kind chosen. Rejected: the chosen `kind` now (T125's case, no scenario).

**Three parts drawn out of the card**, as Tophe asked in PROGRESS when the
form duplicated the row: the cover column, the author lines, the
bookshelf rows, each a presentational component both draw. The top part's
*ISBN* and the field-like look stay the form's own. Rejected: copying the
card's template into the form (two places to change per word); one
"card top" component (the card's top part holds the heading, the form's
the ISBN).

**The language rule lives in `domain/Language.ts`**, as `nameOrder.ts`
holds the `Intl.Collator`: `languageWord(code, locale)`, the component
passing the i18n locale; T127 adds the word to code beside it. Rejected:
a composable in `ui` (a rule of words, no Vue in it).

**The dock is CSS.** Form and dock share one block; the dock, the button
and its message, is `sticky` at the bottom of the content's scroll, above
which the tab bar sits, on `bg`, `container-type: scroll-state`, its
hairline under `@container scroll-state(stuck: bottom)`, which Chrome for
Android has (the rule checked in `dist/` after `npm run build`). The
mockup draws it at rest; docked, the hairline spans the column and the
button keeps the content's side padding, 8 above and below it. Rejected:
an `IntersectionObserver` on a sentinel (script for a look jsdom cannot
see).
