## Goal
The trick works both ways: with the beep on, three taps in a row turn it
off without a sound, and the camera's next read is silent. A row starts
anew once it has worked the trick, so six quick taps turn the beep on,
then off; keeping the state on the phone (T141) builds on this.

## Risks and decisions
**The trick turns the beep off in silence, and sounds only when it turns
it on.** The spec says "the beep sounds once as the answer; the same trick
turns it off again, in silence", and S5 counts no beep. Rejected: a
different sound for off (no document gives one, and the spec says
silence).

**The row starts anew once the trick has worked, on or off**, as the
backlog line says: the tap after the third is the first of a new row,
however soon it comes, so six quick taps turn the beep on, then off.
Rejected: a fourth quick tap turning the beep off (the trick is three
taps, not one more); the taps after the trick counting for nothing until
a slow one (no document says so, and six quick taps would leave the beep
on).

**A guard for the trick worked a third time.** Steps 2 and 3 satisfy it;
it is kept so the row's restart after turning off is held as well as
after turning on, which S5 cannot see.
