## Goal
Tapping *Ajouter à ma bibliothèque* on a form with a missing title, a
number that is not one, or a language word naming no language sends
nothing: each such field says why under it and the first takes the focus.
The add of T123 reads only a draft these rules let through; T125 adds the
volume number without a series to the same rules.

## Risks and decisions
**Ask, then read: `refusalsOf` beside `editionOf`.** The form asks
`refusalsOf` on the tap and calls `editionOf` only when it answers no
refusal, as an aggregate is asked before it changes; `editionOf` keeps its
signature and its tests, reading only drafts the rules let through.
- Rejected: `editionOf` answering an edition or the refusals (rewrites T123's reading, its spec and the form's call for no new case).
- Rejected: the rules in the component (a rule out of the domain, U04).

**The domain answers a code per field; the form picks the first.**
`Refusals` is keyed by the draft's field; the component finds the first
refused in its own `fields` order (the order is the form's, the domain
has none) and maps a code to `isbn.form.refusal.<code>`. T125 adds its
code (*Tome sans série*, worded by the kind in the key, U06) to the same
type.
- Rejected: an ordered list from the domain (the domain would hold the screen's order).
- Rejected: a catalogue key per field (`Nombre invalide` written three times).

**A whole number from 1 is digits alone, of a value from 1**, after the
spaces at its ends: `01` is 1, `1e3`, `+3`, `1.0` and `0` are refused. A
number past what Libris stores is not the form's to refuse: Libris
answers the validation problem and S11's message shows.
- Rejected: `Number(text)` read as an integer (takes `1e3` and `0x10`, the text as typed is no whole number).

**A refusal lasts until the next tap.** "Checked on the add's tap, and not
before": typing in a refused field leaves its outline and message, and
the next tap checks every field afresh, sending when none is refused.
- Rejected: clearing a field's refusal on its input (a check before the tap, not in the spec).

**The first refused field is focused, then brought to the middle**
(`scrollIntoView({ block: "center" })`): the docked button covers the
bottom of the content, so a field aligned to the nearest edge from above
would land under it.
- Rejected: `focus()` alone (the browser's own scroll may leave it under the dock).
- Rejected: `{ block: "nearest" }` as `ReaderChoice` (same reason).
