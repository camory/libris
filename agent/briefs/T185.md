## Goal
The three finds read a problem through one function of `application/`,
and their states share one type for the two failures; the series page and
the edition page draw their failure through one component that picks the
sentence by the failure. The next change to what a find's failure means,
or to how a page words it, is then made once.

## Risks and decisions
**The mapping reads the problem's type; the rejection stays each
composable's `catch`.** `findFailureOf(answer.type)` is what the three
`switch`es share. `useFindBookshelf`'s `catch` also covers its second ask,
the readers', so a helper that awaits the find's promise would not serve
it, and every other composable of the tree reads a rejection in its own
`catch`. Rejected: a function wrapping the ask (does not fit the
bookshelf's two asks); a function taking the whole answer (each found
branch builds its own state).

**The type is `FindFailure`, in `application/FindFailure.ts`, beside
`findFailureOf`.** The file is named after the type it declares and the
function after what it answers, as `domain/SearchQuery.ts` holds
`searchQueryOf` and `infra/api/problemOf.ts` names its function. It sits
in `application/` because it reads the keys the ports answer, which
`domain` never sees. Rejected: `domain/` (the keys would cross into it);
one of the three ports' files (each belongs to one resource); a generic
composable `useFind` (not a use case's sentence, D10).

**The pages pick their words through a component, `FindFailureMessage`.**
Two views share code only through `domain`, `application` or
`ui/components`, and the sentences are the screen's, so a presentational
component taking the failure's status and both sentences, translated by
the caller as `FailureMessage`'s `sentence` is, is the one place both
reach. Rejected: a word-picking function in `application/` (the
application holds no sentence); keys renamed after the status and built
in the template (changes `i18n.ts`, builds a key, a trap of the gotchas,
and still shares nothing); a module under `ui/` for both views (a new
element of the boundaries, a layer decision); the component translating
its own keys (the two pages' keys differ).

**No spec of its own for either new file.** The line is a refactor whose
proof is the existing tests unchanged; each composable's spec already
proves every branch of `findFailureOf`, and each page's spec both
sentences of `FindFailureMessage`, as `SuggestionList` and
`NameStatusWord` are proven through their callers. Rejected: new specs
(cases the line does not ask, repeating the composables' and pages').

**Five moves, five commits.** One commit per composable and per page
keeps each diff a few lines and its proof one row of the table. Rejected:
a split (seven small files, under ten minutes of reading together).
