# T041 — The bookshelf rows of the card, more visible, and the absence

**Backlog line:** Frontend: the bookshelf rows of the card, more visible, and
the absence. Precondition (human): the S4 scenario of no bookshelf of the
reader's expects the absence row, skipped until this task (D07). Each bookshelf
row of the card, between the authors and the field rows, is in `body` semibold
with the book icon at 22 before its words, so the reader in a bookstore sees at
a glance where their copies are; when no bookshelf of the reader holds a copy,
the card shows one row of the same shape, *Dans aucune de vos bibliothèques*,
on an edition the house holds as on one the sources answer; every word from the
`fr` catalogue (U06, U07, U08). Component tests over no copy and one copy read
the weight, the icon and the words; the absence word is asserted in three
places (`agent/GOTCHAS.md`); the fast-entry scenarios stay green with the row
they gain. Placed before T039 so the button lands on the final rows. Un-skips
`S4 The ouvrage is already in a bookshelf, none of the reader's`.

**Serves:** spec `bookshelf` — S4 and its case *none of the reader's* (the card
says *Dans aucune de vos bibliothèques*), the first two paragraphs of *Screen*;
PRD §4.3; decisions D05, D07, D10; screen rules U02, U03, U06, U07, U08. The
precondition is met: `fb09301` (#129).

**First of its kind:** the first **type step worn at a weight other than its
own**: the rows are `text-body font-semibold`, the step's size and line height
with U06's weight, not a new token in `style.css`. Tailwind 4.3.3 emits
`.text-body { font-weight: var(--tw-font-weight, 400) }`, so `font-semibold`
wins whatever the order of the two classes (checked while planning). The
reviewer repeats this line in its verdict.

## Goal
When this task is done, every card of the lookup screen says where the reader's
copies are, or that none of their bookshelves holds one, on a row a glance
catches: the book icon, then the words in semibold. T039 then puts the button
under a card whose rows are final.

## Risks and decisions
**The weight and the icon's size are proven by a command, not by a component
test.** The line asks the component tests to "read the weight"; D07 forbids a
view or component test to assert a class, and jsdom loads no stylesheet, so no
computed weight exists to read. The component cases read what the reader sees
of the change, the icon and the words; the greps of the criteria read the weight
and the 22, as T030 proved its tokens. The PR body says so.
- Rejected: asserting `font-semibold` with `classes()` — a class in a test.
- Rejected: a `<strong>` to query — a tag chosen for a test, and emphasis is
  not what semibold says here.

**`text-body font-semibold`, not a new step.** U06 names the row `body`
semibold: the `body` step worn heavier, not a step U02 lacks. Tailwind resolves
the weight through `--tw-font-weight`, so the two classes compose.
- Rejected: `--text-body-strong` in `style.css` — a step U02 does not list, a
  convention for Tophe to settle.

**The absence is its own entry, `isbn.card.noCopy`, shown when no bookshelf
holds a copy.** The row has no bookshelf to fill a slot and no count; the card
shows it when its bookshelves are none, as it already renders the rows' part
only then.
- Rejected: a zero form of `isbn.card.copies` — the entry is picked per
  bookshelf, and the absence has none.

**The icon is resized by a class.** `IconBook` keeps its 48 for the cover;
the rows draw it with `size-5.5`, as the tab bar draws 22-wide icons at 32.
- Rejected: a `size` prop on the icons — every icon would change for one caller.

**The cover cases count icons.** With a row icon on every card, *a book icon
exists* no longer tells the stand-in from the row; the count does, without
reaching for a class or a tag.

**The skipped S2 and S5 will contradict the card.** Both expect `rows(screen)`
to be `[]` on a card of `9782723488525` with no copy; after this task that card
reads *Dans aucune de vos bibliothèques*, so T039 cannot un-skip S2 as written.
D07 makes that a spec conversation: the implementer leaves both untouched and
writes it in `agent/PROPOSED.md` and the PR body, for Tophe before T039. Same
for `specs/bookshelf.md` *Screen*, whose sentence "No row when the reader's
bookshelves hold none" and state *Found*, "the card without a copies row",
predate #129; this brief follows the newer text of S4, of *Screen*'s first
paragraph, of U06 and of the line. The spec's *Tasks* section lacks T041; the
next backlog plan adds it.
