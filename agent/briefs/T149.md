## Goal
While the reader types an author's name, up to five authors of the house
whose names contain the text show under that author's box, sorted, the
matched part in semibold, and a tap puts one in place of the text; when
Libris does not answer the names, the box works as before. The name taking
the focus brings its box to the top, so the rows show above the keyboard.

## Risks and decisions
**The rows live in `AuthorBox`**, which owns its name, its focus and its
roles' list; the suggestions read the house's names as a prop.
- Rejected: the form drawing rows under each box (it would track which
  box's name has the focus); `AuthorBox` calling the port (D05).

**One block of rows, `SuggestionList`.** T127 settled one copy of their
markup; `AuthorBox` is a second user, so the block becomes a component
with no spec of its own, as `CoverChoice`, proven through its callers;
each caller keeps when it shows them.
- Rejected: a second copy of the markup in `AuthorBox` (two places for
  U04's rows).

**The names reach the box as `string[] | null`**, as `seriesNames` do:
`null` until answered and when Libris does not answer, which T150 needs
to say no word; a `null` suggests nothing.
- Rejected: `[]` on failure (T150 could not tell unknown from none).

**Asked for each lookup, beside the series' names.** An author the last
add created is offered in the next form, as T126 decided for series.
- Rejected: once at the view's setup (step 13's mutation).

**The scroll is the box's, with *Série*'s margin.** On its name's focus
the whole box, name and roles, goes to the top through
`scrollIntoView({ block: "start" })`, its `scroll-mt-28` the 112 px
Tophe tuned for *Série* in #335; 112 + the box's 93 + 4 + five 44 rows
fit the ≈ 500 px the Pixel leaves above the keyboard. Tophe tunes.
- Rejected: flush at the top (#335's finding); the name's input alone
  (the rows sit under the roles' line, so the whole box must show).

**The status check's message** follows `FetchSeriesApi`'s: *The author
names answered <status>*, after the path `/author-names`.
