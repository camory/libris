## Goal
One component of `ui/components`, `RowCover`, draws a row's cover: its
box, its picture, the fall-back on a picture that does not load, and the
book icon standing in for none. `CatalogueRow` and `SeriesRow` draw it,
so the next change to a row's cover is made once.

## Risks and decisions
**The name is `RowCover`.** U07 calls the stand-in that of *a row's
missing cover*, and the component serves the rows alone, the card's cover
being `CoverChoice`. Rejected: `Cover` (the card has one too, of another
size and behaviour); `CatalogueCover` (the series list is a view of the
catalogue as well, and the name does not say which drawing).

**The props are `url` and `alt`, the caller translating the alt.** Each
row builds its alt from its own key and field (`catalogue.cover` with the
title, `series.cover` with the name), as `FailureMessage`'s callers
translate its `sentence`; `url` is the domain's `coverUrl`, `null` for
none. Rejected: passing the `Book` or the `Series` (the component would
branch on what it is given); a `title` prop and one key (the two keys
differ, `{title}` and `{name}`, and `series.cover` is read by the series
page's band too); `label`, `IconButton`'s word (an `img` is named by its
alt).

**The component holds the box and its classes; the row holds its own
layout.** The box's size, radius and background are what U06 fixes for a
row's cover and are identical in both rows, so they move into the root;
neither row adds a class to it. Rejected: a slot for the stand-in (both
draw the same icon).

**Three cases, three red steps, two moves, two commits.** One commit per
row keeps each diff a few lines, its proof that row's specs. Rejected:
one commit for both moves (a red could not be traced to its row); a split
(two files moved, under ten minutes of reading together).
