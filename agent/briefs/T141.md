## Goal
The beep stays as the trick left it on that phone: turned on, it is on
again when Libris is opened again; never turned on, turned off, or the
app's data cleared, it is off. This closes the scan beep's scenarios,
leaving the spec's *Done* to Tophe on the Pixel.

## Risks and decisions
**The port is `BeepSetting`, `isOn()` and `keep(on)`, synchronous.** It
is what the phone holds of the beep, and `Storage` answers at once, so
the composable reads it in its setup and writes it at the trick with no
pending state. Rejected: a method on `Beeper` (the sound and the record
are two browser APIs, two adapters); a promise-answering port (nothing
waits); a generic key-value port (no second setting needs one).

**The adapter is `WebStorageBeepSetting`, in `src/infra/storage`**, named
after the browser API it reaches, as `WebAudioBeeper` and
`ServiceWorkerAppUpdate` are, given its `Storage` as `WebAudioBeeper` is
given its context. Rejected: `LocalStorageBeepSetting` (the class takes
any `Storage`, `bootstrap` choosing `localStorage`).

**One entry, `beep`, holding `on`; off is no entry.** The phone where the
trick was never done, the one turned off and the one whose data was
cleared are then the same state, with nothing to tell apart. The key is
the one Libris keeps on the phone, not a naming scheme; the spec's
`afterEach` already clears the whole storage. Renaming it later turns
every phone's beep off, so step 1 asserts it. Rejected: `"true"` and
`"false"` (two forms of off); a prefixed key such as `libris.beep` (a
scheme no document states, on an origin Libris has alone).

**Storage refused is not handled.** A browser that refuses the page's
storage refuses its cookies too, and then Authelia's session never holds:
Libris does not open at all, so no document describes a lookup screen
without storage. Rejected: a `try` around each access (a case no reader
can reach).

**The scenario hands the real adapter, the others a fake.** The backlog
line puts `WebStorageBeepSetting` over the real `localStorage` in
`ScanBeepScenarios`' `open()`, which S6's two openings and the
`afterEach` written with the spec need. The 13 other files pass
`new FakeBeepSetting()`, off, as they pass `new FakeBeeper()`. Rejected:
the real adapter everywhere (those files do not clear the storage).

**No case for `IsbnView`.** Passing the injected port to the composable
is wiring, proven by S6 over the real view; `mountView` provides the fake
without a parameter, since no view case varies it.
