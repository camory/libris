## Goal
*Série* tells the reader, as they type or once the form opens, whether the
add will land on a series the house holds (*Existante*) or create one
(*Nouvelle*), and says nothing while it cannot know; *Langue* offers the
words of a language as *Série* offers the names. The reader sees the series
an add will reach before tapping it, and types a language by its first
letters.

## Risks and decisions
**Unknown names are `null`.** `useListSeriesNames` holds `null` until Libris
answers and when it does not, so the form tells "the house holds no series"
(`[]`, every text *Nouvelle*) from "Libris did not say" (no word, as S8
asks); suggestions read `null` as none. A form opened before the names land
shows its word when they do.
- Rejected: a `failed` ref beside `names` (two refs that must agree); `[]`
  on failure (S8's *no word* unmet).

**Existante is equality under `nameOrder(locale)`**, the text trimmed: the
`Intl.Collator` at `sensitivity: "base"` is the frontend twin of the ICU
level 1 collation the backend's unique index matches a series with, so the
word says where the add lands. `languageCode` already reads a word this way.
- Rejected: `suggestionsFor`'s character folding (a second rule, unlike the
  backend's for *Ø* or *ß*).

**The rule answers a status, `null` for no word**, so the form picks
`isbn.form.seriesNameStatus.<status>` and draws nothing for `null`; blank
text says no word, since a blank series is sent as none.

**`Language` gains `languageWords(locale)`** (D10: the caller needs the
words of the private `CODES`, and the type gains a door). Each word once,
since the browser names `ak` and `tw` both *akan* and the rows are keyed by
their text. Rejected: exporting `CODES`.

**The check is drawn at 22**, the size U07 gives the icon beside the
`body` semibold words of a card's bookshelf row; `2-filled.jpg` draws it
smaller, a hand-check finding for Tophe to settle in U07. Rejected: 16, a
size U07 does not list.

**No scroll for *Langue*.** #335 leaves the scroll to *Série* alone, tuned
on the Pixel, and neither S9, the backlog line nor *Done* asks one for
*Langue*; step 18 keeps it so.

**One block of rows for both fields.** T126 kept the rows in
`EditionForm` for one user and named T127, the second, to share them;
`EditionForm.vue` holds one copy of their markup, whether through a
component of `ui/components` with no spec of its own (as `CoverChoice`) or
the `v-for` of fields: the implementer's refactor.
