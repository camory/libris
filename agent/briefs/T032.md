# T032 — The card in the words of its kind

**Backlog line:** Frontend: the card in the words of its kind. Precondition
(human): `frontend/src/scenario/KindScenarios.spec.ts`, one skipped test bearing
the exact title of `S1 A manga`, against `contracteer mock` (D07).
`vitest.global-setup.ts` pins `v0.5.0`, the only contract edit of the task
(D04). `domain` and `infra/api`: `SourceEdition` gains its `kind`, read from the
answer by `FetchIsbnApi` (D05). `ui`: `SourceEditionCard` writes the series line
with *tome* for a livre and a manga and *album* for a BD; the authors are
grouped by their whole set of roles — the names alone on one line, separated by
commas, when every author shares one set, otherwise one line per author with
their role words; the role words follow the kind, texte and illustration for a
livre, scénario and dessin for a BD and a manga, couleurs et traduction
unchanged; every word from the `fr` catalogue (U04, U07, U08). Component tests
over the three kinds and over authors whose role sets differ; a word the card
shows is asserted in three files (`agent/GOTCHAS.md`). Realises S1, S2, S3;
un-skips `S1 A manga`.

**Serves:** spec `kind` — S1 *A manga*, S2 *A BD*, S3 *A book*, their second
`Then` line ("the card uses the words of a…"), the *Screen* section whole and
the *Contract* section (release `v0.5.0`); PRD §3 for ouvrage, série, tome and
a role as an activity; decisions D01, D04, D05, D07, D08, D10, D11 and screen
rules U02, U06, U08. S4 is backend-only and was T031's.

**First of its kind:** the first **word on screen chosen by another field of
the answer**. Until now a word came from the field it renders — `role.WRITER`
from the author's `role`, `language.fr` from `language` — or was fixed on the
screen. Here the kind of the ouvrage decides how a role and a volume are named,
and two conventions follow that no document states yet. *Where the choice
lives*: in the key of the catalogue entry, `role.MANGA.WRITER` and
`isbn.card.series.MANGA`, so the component picks an entry with two codes the
API answered and holds no branch and no French word — U06's "keyed by the code
the API answers", with one code more. *What a kind that shares a word carries*:
its own entry all the same, `role.BD.COLOURIST` and `role.MANGA.COLOURIST` both
spelling *couleurs* and the manga's series pattern repeating the livre's, so
the `fr` block reads as the spec's table, one column per kind, and a kind whose
word changes is one line, never a shared entry to split first. The reviewer
repeats this line in its verdict and Tophe reviews the pull request
interactively before merging, amending `docs/DESIGN.md` with what the review
settles.

## Goal
When this task is done, the card of the lookup screen names things as the kind
of the ouvrage names them: *One piece · tome 1* over a manga and *Astérix ·
album 1* over a BD, *scénario* and *dessin* under a comic strip where a livre
reads *texte* and *illustration*, and the authors who share one set of roles
named alone on one line — *Eiichirō Oda* under One Piece, where the card says
*scénario, dessin* today. The frontend is verified against release `v0.5.0` of
the contract and the one frontend scenario of `specs/kind.md` runs un-skipped.
It matters now because T031 put `kind` on the wire and the phase's *Done* asks
Tophe to scan a manga, a BD and a novel on the Pixel and read three cards that
differ only in their words.

## Risks and decisions
- **The catalogue is keyed by the kind, and every kind carries its full set of
  words, decided here.** `isbn.card.series` becomes `isbn.card.series.BOOK`,
  `.BD` and `.MANGA`, each holding the whole pattern (`{name} · tome {volume}`
  twice, `{name} · album {volume}` once), and `role` becomes `role.BOOK.*`,
  `role.BD.*` and `role.MANGA.*`, each with the four roles, `couleurs` and
  `traduction` repeated. U06 already keys a word by the code the API answers;
  this adds the one code more that decides it, so the component picks an entry
  and never branches. Grouping the two comic kinds under one shared name would
  invent a word no document uses and would have to be split the first time a
  manga reads otherwise than a BD; six repeated words are cheaper than that.
  The reviewer should weigh the repetition; if Tophe prefers another shape,
  `docs/DESIGN.md` is where it is settled.
- **The grouping stays in the component, decided here.** `authorLines` already
  groups the authors by name in a computed; the spec's rule adds the comparison
  of the role sets, and the words it needs are the catalogue's, which only the
  `ui` layer reaches. A pure function in `domain` would have to answer role
  codes and let the component regroup them for its lines — one layer more for
  no assertion the component test cannot make. The backlog line puts the
  grouping under `ui` and that is where it goes. The day a second screen names
  authors, the shared part moves to `domain` with its own spec
  (`agent/PROPOSED.md`).
- **The role sets are compared on a canonical key, decided here.** Two authors
  whose roles arrive in different orders are one set and must give one line of
  names; the answer's order is the BnF's and nothing promises it. So the
  comparison sorts the role codes and the display keeps the order the answer
  gave, which is what makes *Eiichirō Oda · scénario, dessin* read in that
  order when a case needs the words.
- **`SourceEdition.kind` is not nullable, decided here.** The contract makes
  `kind` required and never null, and the backend always names one — Open
  Library answers `BOOK`, the merge takes the first source's kind as it takes
  the title — so a `| null` on the frontend would be a branch no answer can
  reach and a default the card would have to invent. An older backend that answers no
  `kind` is the drift the pinned mock exists to catch; the frontend does not
  guard against it, exactly as it does not guard against a missing `title`.
- **The fixtures of the BD and the livre are built inside the card's spec,
  decided here.** Only that spec needs them, and the tree's convention is that
  `src/fixture` holds what two files share; the cases spread `onePiece1` the
  way the file already does for `barelyKnown` and `partly`. The names come from
  the spec — Astérix with Goscinny and Uderzo, Erckmann and Chatrian for the
  livre — so the words on the screen and the words of the spec read alike.
- **Only `vitest.global-setup.ts` is formatted, decided here.** `npm run format`
  rewrites three files on `main`: this one (the long pinned URL, the follow-up
  `agent/PROPOSED.md` asks the next task touching it to clear),
  `src/ui/views/home/HomeView.spec.ts`, which this task does not touch, and
  `src/scenario/KindScenarios.spec.ts`, which D07 forbids this task to change
  beyond the `.skip`. Run `npx prettier --write vitest.global-setup.ts` and
  never the repository-wide script; Prettier is not in the gate, so nothing
  else is at risk.
- **The mock answers the contract's example for `9782723488525`, measured on
  2026-09-19.** `contracteer mock` over `v0.5.0` answers that ISBN with
  `kind: MANGA` and the rest of `ONE_PIECE_1` unchanged, which is what makes
  *S1 A manga* provable; any other ISBN gets a generated kind, which is why no
  case may assert a value against the mock.
- **A word the card shows is asserted in three files.** The role words leaving
  the One Piece card are already out of `FastEntryScenarios.spec.ts` (the human
  precondition did it) and were never in `IsbnView.spec.ts`; *Eiichirō Oda*,
  *One piece* and the tome stay on the card and in all three. Grep a word over
  `src/` before calling the change done — that gotcha is what a forgotten third
  file costs.
