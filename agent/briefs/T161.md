## Risks and decisions
**The cross unmounts the form; nothing resets it.** The page's form and
card already sit in one `v-if` chain, so `editing` back to false drops the
form with its edits and the next pencil mounts one that fills its text from
the answer at setup, as the lookup's cross does in `IsbnView`. Rejected: a
`reset()` exposed by `EditionForm` (a public method for what remounting
already does); keeping the form mounted and hidden (the edits would come
back, S8's last line red).

**The card is the answer the page holds; no request on the cross.** The
form edits copies of its own and never `state.book`, so the card drawn
after the cross is the answer as Libris gave it; no case asserts the
absence of a request, which no scenario states. Rejected: `refresh(id)` on
the cross (a request no scenario asks for, which would only redraw the
same card).

**No question before the edits go.** The spec says so (S8, edition-form
S13), and U04's row action asks only before destroying what is stored;
unsaved edits are not.

**The cross stays enabled in this task.** Nothing runs while the form shows
until T162 sends the save; disabling it during the save is T162's, with its
busy state.
