ignoring case, accents and the spaces at its ends, whatever the roles of
either, is refused: the `danger` outline, *Auteur en double* under it,
nothing sent, and when it is the first refused of the form its name takes
the focus. The last case of S8, so the form refuses every author the spec
names before anything reaches Libris.

## Risks and decisions
**The match is the screen's, `nameOrder(locale)` on trimmed names.** It is
the rule *Existant* reads (`nameStatus`), so two boxes that both read
*Existant* for one author of the house are doubles; the empty-name
exemption reads the name through `text`, as T151's and T148's.
- Rejected: `suggestionsFor`'s character folding (a containment rule, not
  an equality); a new folding of its own (a third reading of one rule).

**`"twice"` wins over `"missingRole"` on one box.** A box is one part
with one message (S8: *its message under it*). A doubled box is mended by
removing or renaming it, which settles its roles too; its name, not its
roles, is what the reader must look at, so the name takes the focus. A
small, reversible choice no document makes.
- Rejected: both messages under one box (two answers at once, against
  U05); the missing role first (focuses the roles of a box that goes).

**The doubled box is focused through the existing `focusName()`.** T151
named it for this. Its focus runs `nameFocused`, which brings the box to
the top of what the keyboard leaves, as S6 says of any name taking the
focus, and opens the house's suggestions: the box is in view, and the
form's other refusals keep their centre scroll.
- Rejected: a `focusName` that also scrolls to the centre (two scrolls on
  one focus, the second undoing S6's); a third exposed method (a wider
  door for the same act).

**The code is `twice`, its words `isbn.form.authors.refusal.twice`,**
the value T151's brief reserved, beside `missingRole`.
