## Goal
One component of `ui/components`, `FailureMessage`, draws the message of
U05, and the fourteen places that draw it by hand draw it instead. The
next change to the message (its size, its colour, its role) is then made
once, and the next screen that fails cannot draw it differently.

## Risks and decisions
**The name is `FailureMessage`, its prop `sentence`.** U05 calls the
drawing a *message* and its text *one sentence*, and the backlog line
*the failure message*; the caller translates, as `NameStatusWord`'s
callers translate its `word`. Rejected: `DangerMessage` (names a colour
role, not what the reader is told); `Message` (U05's heading groups three
drawings, and several views already hold a `message` computed); a default
slot (nothing would type the sentence, and the callers would keep writing
the inside).

**Each caller keeps its spacing on the tag, the component holds only what
U05 fixes.** The spacing (`pt-5`, `mt-1`, `relative -mt-1.5 px-3.5
pb-3.5`, a bound `mt-5`) is the caller's layout, and a single-root
component passes `class` and `:class` through to its root. Rejected: a
spacing prop (an API for what Vue already gives); moving the spacing into
the component (fourteen variants of one drawing).

**The spec asserts the sentence and the icon, not the size nor the
colour.** Those are classes and an icon's size prop, which D07 keeps out
of a component test; they are carried over unchanged from the sites and
read by the reviewer in the template diff. Rejected: a case on
`props("size")` (no spec of the tree asserts an icon's size; it would be
the first such convention, not this task's to set).

**Fourteen moves, fourteen commits.** One commit per file keeps each diff
a few lines, its proof the specs of its row; the gate runs once, at the
end. Rejected: one commit for every move (a red in the gate could not be
traced to its file); a split of the task (the moves are one mechanical
change, under ten minutes of reading together).
