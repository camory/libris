## Goal
While the camera is open on the lookup screen, it alone fills the space
under the ISBN field; closed, the card or the form the reader left shows
again exactly as left, edits included. The form being where a reader types
for a while, opening the camera by mistake must not cost them their edits.

## Risks and decisions
**The answer is kept alive across the camera, with Vue's `KeepAlive`.**
S14 reads no *Titre* label while the camera is open, so the form must
leave the document, and the form holds its edits in its own state (it
fills its text once, at setup). `KeepAlive` keeps the card or the form
the camera hides, its edits, its tapped cover, its unfolded summary and
whatever later tasks add (T124's refusals), with no component changed.
- Rejected: `v-show` or `hidden` (`getByLabelText` ignores visibility; S14 would stay red).
- Rejected: the form's draft lifted into the view (`v-model`) (rewrites the form's props and spec, and every later field state would have to follow).
- Rejected: only the form kept (the card's unfolded summary folds again; U06 says "for good").

**Kept across the camera only.** Everything else that changes the answer
still builds afresh: the pencil and the cross (S13, T128's decision that
the form is remounted on each pencil), a lookup typed or read (the
skeleton replaces the answer, as today), the add's card. Traps the
implementer meets: `KeepAlive` caches whatever leaves it, so S13 is what
proves the cross's form is not reused; it keeps exactly one component
child, so a branch drawing the card and `DockedAdd` together is a
fragment it does not cache, and the dock is drawn outside it, hidden
while the camera is open; it must stay mounted while the camera is open,
so whatever holds it draws no space then (U03, by hand).

**The card's case is the view's, the form's is S14.** The backlog line
gives the card to the lookup screen's component test; the form's edits
are S14's to prove, so S14 is step 2's red rather than a second view case
repeating it.
