# T030 — The tab bar

**Backlog line:** Frontend: the tab bar. `ui/components`: the tab bar of U03 —
on `surface` with a `border` hairline above, two tabs of equal width, *Accueil*
with a house icon to `/` and *Ajouter* with a plus icon to `/isbn`, each its
icon over its label, 56 tall, `accent` on the screen shown and `muted`
otherwise, the bottom safe area under it; every word from the `fr` catalogue
(U04, U07, U08), the two icons beside `IconBarcode`. `App.vue` shows it under
the footer on every screen, the active tab read from the route; the home page
keeps its link to `/isbn`. The same task draws `HomeView` to U02 and U03, its
title and padding on the scale's steps, and gives the lookup screen a
full-height column so the camera view fills the space between the *Chercher*
button and the tab bar, in place of the fixed `aspect-[3/4]` ratio. Component
test over the active tab per route; the lookup screen's tests unchanged.
Realises the *Screen* section of the spec, the bar the lookup screen is reached
from; un-skips nothing.

**Serves:** spec `fast-entry` — the *Screen* section ("Route `/isbn`, reached
from the home page's link and from the tab bar", "at the bottom the tab bar,
*Accueil* and *Ajouter*, the latter active on this screen") and its *Camera
open* state ("the camera view, with corner brackets, fills the space between
the button and the tab bar, centred"), with the mockups
`specs/fast-entry/1-ready.jpg` and `2-camera-open.jpg`; PRD §3 for the words
of the screen; decisions D05, D07, D10; rules U01, U02, U03, U04, U07 and U08
of `docs/DESIGN.md`. No scenario of `specs/fast-entry.md` is realised or
un-skipped: the bar is how the reader reaches the screen the scenarios already
prove.

**First of its kind:** the first **navigation chrome**, and with it four
conventions no document states yet. It is the first component that shows the
reader where they are: the active tab is marked `aria-current="page"`, which
`RouterLink` writes by itself on an exactly-active link, and the component test
asserts it through `getByRole("link", { current: "page" })` — the only way D07's
"text and roles, never tags or classes" can reach an active state. It is the
first component under `ui/components` that imports `vue-router` (allowed:
`eslint.config.ts` lets every non-`domain` element import an external module)
and the first that reads something other than its props. It is the first use of
the phone's bottom safe area, and so the first edit of `index.html` since the
scaffold (`viewport-fit=cover`, without which `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` is
always `0`). And it is the first shell that holds its own height so that a
block of a screen can grow into what is left, which is what replaces the
camera's fixed ratio. The reviewer repeats this line in its verdict and Tophe
reviews the pull request interactively before merging, amending
`docs/DESIGN.md` with what the review settles.

## Goal
When this task is done, every screen of Libris ends with the same two tabs,
*Accueil* and *Ajouter*, the one the reader is on in `accent` and the other in
`muted`, over the padding the phone's home indicator needs; the home page is
drawn with the type scale and the column of U02 and U03 instead of the
scaffold's Tailwind defaults; and the camera view of the lookup screen fills
the space that is actually left between the *Chercher* button and the bar
rather than a 3∶4 rectangle that either overflows or leaves a hole. It matters
now because fast entry is done and its *Done* was checked on the Pixel through
the home page's link alone (`agent/PROGRESS.md`, 2026-09-16): the spec's screen
is reached from a bar that does not exist yet, and every screen of every later
feature is reached from it too.

## Rules in play
- **D05** — Vue 3 with `<script setup>`, Tailwind only, hand-written, no UI
  library and no icon package; `ui/components` are presentational and
  `ui/views` own the ports. The bar takes no prop and emits nothing: it reads
  the route through `RouterLink`, which is `vue-router`, an external module the
  boundaries rules allow it (`eslint.config.ts` is not edited). `App.vue` is
  the shell (`ui-shell`), which may import `ui/components`; nothing imports a
  view but the router.
- **D07** — `cd frontend && npm test` is the gate, run plainly as the last
  command of its call. A component test mounts and asserts what the reader
  sees, text and roles, never a tag, a class, a colour or a pixel; a view test
  mounts with the real i18n; every body is `// Given`, `// When`, `// Then`
  unless it is a single statement. The scenario file
  `src/scenario/FastEntryScenarios.spec.ts` is not edited — this task un-skips
  nothing and its eight cases must stay green as they are. One test creates the
  application through `createLibrisApp` over fake ports; it is where the shell
  itself is proven.
- **D10** — TypeScript strict, ESLint with the boundaries rules and Prettier;
  code and identifiers in English, the screen's words in French; Conventional
  Commits with the scope `frontend`, branch `task/T030-tab-bar`, the trailer
  `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` on every commit.
- **U01** — the bar is `bg-surface` with a `border` hairline above it; the
  active tab is `text-accent` and the other `text-muted`. Role names only: no
  slate, teal or red shade in a template, and no new colour role — the six of
  `style.css` cover this task.
- **U02** — the tab step is 12 / medium, semibold on the active tab, and it is
  **missing from `src/ui/style.css`**: this task adds `--text-tab` (with its
  line height and its 500 weight) beside the other steps, and the template
  writes `text-tab`, never a size. The home page's title takes the `page title`
  step (`text-page-title`), which replaces the scaffold's `text-2xl font-bold`.
- **U03** — one column, designed at 390, capped at 480 and centred. The bar:
  `surface`, a `border` hairline above, one tab per top-level screen, equal
  widths, *Accueil* (a house) and *Ajouter* (a plus), each tab its icon over
  its label, 56 tall, `accent` on the screen shown and `muted` otherwise, and
  under it the bottom safe area, 16 at least. The footer sits under the content
  and above the bar on every screen. Header, footer and bar stay put; the
  content is the part that scrolls. The home page's padding comes from this
  rule: 20 at the sides, 20 above the title, 12 below the header, 20 between
  blocks.
- **U04** — every control is at least 44 tall and wide: a tab is 56 and each is
  half the width. A tab is a link, not a button, and its word carries its
  meaning, so it is not an icon button and carries no `aria-label`. The lookup
  screen's field, button and icon button do not change.
- **U07** — `IconHome` and `IconPlus`, the two names U07 already lists, each its
  own component under `ui/components/icons`, an inline SVG on the 24 grid,
  stroke 1.8, round caps and joins, `currentColor`, no fill, drawn like
  `IconBarcode` and `IconClose` beside them. An icon next to a word is
  decoration: `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **U08** — the two words are *Accueil* and *Ajouter*, exactly as U03 writes
  them, and they live in the `fr` catalogue: no French string in a component.
  They are labels of one word, so no full stop. *Ajouter* is the tab; *Ajouter
  un ouvrage* stays the title of the lookup screen and the wording of the home
  page's link.

## Risks and decisions
- **The active tab is `aria-current="page"`, and the test asserts it through
  `getByRole("link", { current: "page" })`, decided here.** D07 forbids
  asserting a class or a colour, so the colour U03 asks for cannot itself be
  the proof; the state behind it can. `RouterLink` writes that attribute on an
  exactly-active link by itself (`ariaCurrentValue`, default `page`), so this
  costs no production code and is what a screen reader announces. The colour is
  then written on the same exactly-active state, never on
  `router-link-active`, which `/` would match on every screen. This is part of
  the *First of its kind* line.
- **The bar is a `<nav>` with no accessible name, decided here.** A name would
  be a French sentence that no document writes and U08 forbids inventing one in
  a component; with a single `<nav>` on the page, the two link words are the
  whole announcement. If Tophe wants one, it is a catalogue entry and a line
  in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
- **The two icons are 22, like every icon of this tree, decided here.** U07
  fixes only two sizes, 22 beside a word and 48 for an empty state; a tab is an
  icon beside (above) a word, and 22 over a 12 px label fills 56 with the
  spacing U03 gives a control and what belongs to it.
- **The catalogue keys are `tabs.home` and `tabs.add`, decided here**, one
  section per screen or piece of chrome as `home`, `isbn`, `language` and
  `role` already are; `home.add` stays the wording of the home page's link,
  *Ajouter un ouvrage*, which is a different string from the tab's *Ajouter*.
- **The home page's greeting becomes the header's second line, decided here.**
  U03 gives a header a title and one hint sentence under it; the home page has
  no hint and this task may not invent one (`CLAUDE.md`: no product behaviour
  the spec or the PRD does not describe), so *Bonjour {name}* takes that place
  in the `body` step, `muted`, keeping its words and its absence of a full stop
  (U08: a label of a few words). The link to `/isbn` stays in the content, in
  the `body` step and `accent`. The home page has no spec: everything here is
  the scaffold of Phase 0 redrawn to the rules, and the day a spec describes
  that screen it may say something else.
- **The shell holds the viewport's height and the router view is what scrolls,
  decided here.** It is what U03 asks for ("header, footer and tab bar stay
  put", "this is the part that scrolls") and the only way the camera view can
  fill "the space between the button and the tab bar" once the ratio is gone.
  The risk to watch on the Pixel and in the pull request: a long card, the
  *Found* state with a summary, must still scroll to its end above the footer
  and the bar, and the address bar of a mobile browser must not cut the bar off
  — `dvh`, not `vh`, is what the height is written in. If it cannot be made to
  work without a fixed or sticky bar, say so in the pull request body rather
  than dropping the rule.
- **The order is: the bar first, the two redraws last.** The bar and its icons
  are new files that no test of the tree sees; the two redraws touch screens
  whose tests must stay green untouched. Keeping them last leaves a green tree
  behind every commit and makes the one that can red the scenarios obvious.
- **`viewport-fit=cover` is an edit of `index.html`, not of a document.** No
  decision of `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` covers the page shell, and without it the
  safe area is always zero, so U03's last line would be unverifiable on the
  phone. It is one attribute; it is named in the pull request body.
- **The gate runs where Node 24 and `contracteer` are** (D08). `npm test`
  starts the mock from its global setup; on a box without the CLI it runs
  inside the sandbox image (`agent/GOTCHAS.md`). No duration figure is quoted
  here: the run reports what it measured.
