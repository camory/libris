an author the house holds (*Existant*, after a check in `accent`) or
create one (*Nouveau*, in `muted`), as *Série* already says *Existante* or
*Nouvelle*; while the house's names are unknown, no word shows. This is
the last of S6 on the frontend, and the word is what tells the reader a
respelled name lands on the house's author.

## Risks and decisions
**One rule, `nameStatus` in `src/domain/NameStatus.ts`.** PRD §3 matches
an author's name as it matches a series', and the backend keeps both
under one collation; `seriesNameStatus` already holds that match, and its
body names no series. Moved and renamed in a refactor commit of its own,
its six cases with it, so the rule and its proof stay one.
- Rejected: `AuthorBox` calling `seriesNameStatus` (an author read
  through a series' name); a second function `authorNameStatus` (two
  copies of one match); `suggestionsFor`'s folding (T127's rejection, a
  rule unlike the backend's).

**One markup for the word, `NameStatusWord`.** *Série*'s `statusLooks`
and its span are the first copy; `AuthorBox` is the second, so the word
becomes a component with no spec of its own, proven through
`EditionForm.spec.ts` and `AuthorBox.spec.ts`, as T149 did for
`SuggestionList`. The caller translates the word, so the component never
branches on whose name it is.
- Rejected: a second `statusLooks` in `AuthorBox` (two places for U04's
  word); a prop naming the field and the component building the key (a
  branch the key already carries).

**The word sits in the line, not over the field.** The name's line is
already a flex row, textbox then trash; the word goes between them,
`shrink-0`, so the text box narrows before it, where *Série*'s word is
laid over its box with `pr-32` because that box has no row.
- Rejected: an absolute overlay with a right padding sized for word and
  trash together (two lengths to keep in step by hand).

**The word is `AuthorBox`'s**, which already holds the name and the
house's names; no prop is added and the form is unchanged.
- Rejected: the form computing a status per box (it would read every
  box's name).
