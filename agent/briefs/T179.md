digits alone (`14`) reads its series and tome, as one writing `tome 14`

into the fast entry form with their series filled, where today Tophe types

## Risks and decisions
**Digits alone means the whole `$h`, as written.** The line reads *a tome
of digits alone*, and `UnimarcRecord.value` hands the subfield untrimmed,
so `14` is a tome and ` 14`, `14 bis`, `-3` are not; a match over the whole
text keeps a year or a part's number inside a longer `$h` from becoming a
tome. Rejected: the first number anywhere in `$h` (reads `partie 3` as a
tome of a series named after the title); trimming first (no record seen
needs it).

**The new rule lives in `tomeOf`.** It is the one reader of `200 $h`,
already unit-tested, and `titleStatementSeries` keeps calling it, so the
461 still wins over the title statement and the provisional record still
reads `tome 7`. Rejected: a second helper for the bare form beside
`tomeOf` (two readers of one subfield, one caller choosing between them).

**One recording, read whole in `BnfSourceTest`.** The line makes the
recording the red; comparing the whole preview, as the provisional record
does, also shows that nothing else of this record is misread. Rejected: a
hand-written body (gotcha: a hand-written body is not a recording) and a
scenario of fast entry (no scenario is edited in *Tidy*; the adapter is
where the value is computed).
