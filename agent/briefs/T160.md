## Risks and decisions
**No pencil on an ouvrage with no ISBN.** The spec puts an ouvrage with no
ISBN out of its scope, and the form shows the ISBN under its label and
takes it as text (`isbn13: string`). A book with no ISBN is reachable only
by an add sent by hand. Every fixture of `Books.ts` has none, so the cards
of the other page cases and scenarios keep their buttons as they are.
Rejected: a pencil on every ouvrage (a form showing an absent ISBN, which
no document describes).

**`EditionForm`'s `edition` widens to `SourceEdition | Book`**, as the
card's prop already is: the form reads the fields the two share, its ISBN
coming from `isbn13`, so the page hands its book as it is. Rejected: a
`SourceEdition` built from the book in the view (a mapping the form does
not need).

**The form's button words follow a required `action`.** The lookup's form
adds, the page's saves, and both may be of an edition the house holds, so
`held` cannot tell them apart; a required prop makes `vue-tsc` name each
caller. `DockedAdd` takes the same prop and picks its words from the
catalogue; T160 needs only *Enregistrer*, T162 adds the busy word, T163 the
failure. Rejected: a label passed as text (the component chooses its words
from keys, U08); a slot for the dock (the dock calls the form's own check).

**`DockedAdd` keeps its name in this task.** Renaming it, its `adding` and
its `add` touches the lookup for no behaviour; the item goes to
`agent/PROPOSED.md` for the moment T162 gives the save its busy state.
Rejected: renaming now (a second concern in the diff).

**The page lists the house's names on each tap of the pencil.** The spec's
form is the add's, whose *Série* and authors suggest the house's names and
say *Existante* or *Nouvelle*; `null` names would silently drop that half of
the form. The list is asked on the tap, as the lookup asks it on each
accepted lookup, so a form opened after a save reads the names of then.
Rejected: names left `null` on the page (the form less than the add's);
asked once on the page's load (stale after a save).

**The cross is drawn with no tap.** S8 is T161's, which gives it its
handler and its test; a handler written here would have no red to motivate
it.
