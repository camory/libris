## Goal
`/catalogue/series/{id}` exists: skeletons while the series comes, then
*Série introuvable.* when Libris answers no such series or the id is not
one, over contract `v0.13.0`. The series port then holds the find the
page needs, so T104 to T108 fill the page with no port change.

## Risks and decisions
**The deploy precondition is Tophe's.** As T087 and T096: no run can see
what is deployed; the brief is written on T102 deployed, since Tophe
launches the loop. The PR body says merging is not deploying.

**The composable knows loading and not found alone.** `found` comes red
with T104, `unavailable` with T108; until T104 any answer reads as no such
series, on an address no link reaches. Rejected: `found` now (no test).

**The book mapping is written once.** The `200` answers `Book`s, the
catalogue's; in step 3's refactor the mapping leaves `FetchCatalogueApi`
for `infra/api/bookOf.ts`, beside `problemOf`, and both clients call it,
`FetchCatalogueApi.spec` unchanged in its cases. Likewise the book's
expected shape, with `areCopies`, leaves `FetchCatalogueApi.spec` for
`EditionShapes.ts` as `aBook`. Rejected: a second copy of either in the
series' files.

**The fake gains a second parameter.** Every `new FakeSeriesApi([])`
stays; the find defaults to a rejection, as `FakeBookshelvesApi`'s; S1's
two fakes become one, since `LibrisPorts` has one slot. Rejected: an
answers object (every caller rewritten for one method).

**Names.** The view `SeriesPageView` in `ui/views/seriesPage`, its words a
`seriesPage` block of the catalogue: a folder and a block per view, named
after it, as `bookshelf` and `series`; `series` is the list's. The
skeletons `PageHintSkeleton`, beside `PageTitleSkeleton`, and
`FieldRowSkeleton`, a row of a card's fields. Rejected: the page under
`ui/views/series` (one view per folder).

**S5 un-skipped last, a guard**, as in T096: by step 14 it passes.
