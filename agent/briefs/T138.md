## Goal
Three taps on *Ajouter un ouvrage* turn the beep on and sound it; from then
on, while the lookup screen stays open, each camera read beeps before
Libris answers, through a Web Audio adapter that plays the spec's tone.
The pace (T139), turning it off (T140) and keeping it on the phone (T141)
build on this skeleton.

## Risks and decisions
**The port gains `ready()`, called as the camera opens.** The camera
opens at the screen's mount, right after the tab's tap, and on the
scanner's tap: `openCamera` is where both meet, so the beep is readied
there, on or off. #352 allows the method and the fake's count. Rejected:
the adapter listening for taps on the document (a global read, and any
tap, not the camera's); readying only while on (the view reading the
composable's state, a third function).

**The tone is one oscillator over a periodic wave**, the 1000 Hz
fundamental and its partials at 3000 Hz and 5000 Hz at their relative
levels, the browser normalising the wave's peak to full scale. Rejected:
three oscillators each through a gain (an absolute level no document
gives, and a sum that clips past 1 unless chosen for it).

**The context is built on first use, through a factory.** `ready()` and
`beep()` build it when none exists; `ready()` resumes it. Rejected:
building it in `bootstrap` (suspended before any tap, and a
`ReferenceError` in the smoke case, jsdom having no `AudioContext`).

**The state lives in the composable `IsbnView` holds**, so leaving the
lookup screen forgets the beep until T141 keeps it on the phone; S1 to
S7 never leave the screen. Rejected: a Pinia store (D05 keeps Pinia for
the session and the offline catalogue); a module variable (leaks between
cases, and T141 replaces it).

**`useBeepAtEachRead` exposes two functions**, `tap()` and `read()`, as
`useFindCatalogueEdition` exposes `find` and `refresh`, and no state:
the screen draws nothing of it. Rejected: two composables sharing a state
the view would hand between them.

**The PR title is shortened**: `feat(frontend): T138 the trick turns the
beep on at the camera's read` is 69 characters; the task's title runs
past 72.

**Thirteen scenario files gain one line**, as when `copiesApi` joined in
T134: `vue-tsc` refuses a ports object without the port.
