**Serves:** spec `covers` S3 (both cases) and its *Added* state; PRD §4.1;

shows and keeps that choice to itself. Tophe amended U06 on 2026-10-06
(#208): the card gives out the source of the cover it shows.

**First of its kind:** the first **event that carries a value out of a
component, given out by the component on its own** (on mount, when a cover
fails), not only on a reader's tap; and the first view that keeps what a
component reports and hands it to a use case. `AppUpdateBanner`'s `update`
is the only event so far: no payload, emitted on a click.

## Goal
The add sends Libris the source of the cover the card shows, or null for the
stand-in, so that the worker fetches the picture the reader recognised. The
card keeps its cover and its name once the ouvrage is added.

## Risks and decisions
**The card gives the source out through an event, `coverSource`, with a
`string | null` payload.** Tophe chose on 2026-10-06 that the card sends the
source out. U06 says one thing goes out and D05 says "events out". So an
event is the plain form, and the card keeps its choice.
- Rejected: `defineModel`. It adds a prop in that the view could set, and
  that would share the card's choice with the view.
- Rejected: the view reading the card's state through a template ref and
  `defineExpose`. The parent would reach into the child.

**The card gives the source out on mount and each time it changes.** Three
candidates can load with no failure and no tap, and the add must still send
*inventaire.io*. So the first source goes out when the card mounts. The card
remounts for each lookup, behind the skeleton, so each answer starts over
without the view resetting anything.
- Rejected: giving it out only on a tap or a failure. The common case, the
  first cover kept, would send null.

**`add` takes `coverSource` as a required second parameter.** The view is
the only caller, and the type check then catches a call that forgets the
source. Otherwise null would be sent without any warning. The port keeps
its optional parameter: the port and its fake came with the spec (D07), and
the client's spec calls it without a source.
- Rejected: an optional parameter on the composable, which would let the
  view forget the source without a type error.
- Rejected: making the port's parameter required, which edits the spec's
  fake for nothing S3 needs.

**The cover stays after the add because the card is not remounted.** The
add only changes the card's `copies` prop, and the card keeps its state.
Step 8 is the guard on this.
