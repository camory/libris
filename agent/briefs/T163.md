## Goal
A save Libris refuses or does not answer leaves the page's form open, as
the reader left it, with *Erreur lors de l'enregistrement, veuillez
réessayer plus tard.* under the docked *Enregistrer*. That closes S9 and
the held-edition-edit phase's frontend.

## Risks and decisions
**Every problem and every rejection is `notSaved`.** S9 names one sentence
for the validation problem, the not-found problem and no answer, so the
composable keeps one outcome, as `notAdded` does. The step proves the
validation problem and the rejection; the not-found problem is proven by
S9's own case over the same branch. Rejected: a `notFound` outcome (no
screen tells it apart); the page catching the rejection (a rule in the
view, and the form's handler would need a `try`).

**The message is the dock's, its word chosen by `action`.** `DockedAdd`
already draws `failed` under its button, 8 under it inside the docked
block, and picks its idle and busy words from `action`; its error word
joins the same `words` record. Rejected: a second message drawn by the
page under the form (outside the dock, so not docked with the button, and
two places drawing one answer).

**The message stays across the cross until the next save.** The cross
drops the edits and the form; the save's state is left `notSaved`, so the
form the next pencil opens shows the message again under *Enregistrer*,
as the lookup's card keeps *Erreur lors de l'ajout…* across its own cross.
U05 keeps an answer until the next one replaces it, and S8 speaks of the
fields only. Rejected: resetting the state on the pencil (a second public
function on the composable, or rebuilding it in a `shallowRef`, for a
behaviour no scenario asks). The PR body names it for Tophe's check; a
change is an `agent/PROPOSED.md` item, not this task.

**The scenario cases are guards, last.** Steps 2 to 4 make the three S9
cases green and the form's own check already makes the fourth green, so
each un-skip is a guard with its mutation, not a red. Rejected: the
un-skip first, red across three cycles (one step is one cycle).
