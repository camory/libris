## Goal
The series page reads, under its header, the tomes the reader owns and
the ones missing between 1 and the highest owned, and lists a row for
each run of missing tomes in its place among the editions. The card is
the one T106 adds the authors and the publishers to.

## Rules in play
- D05 — the runs, the gaps and their place among the editions are pure
  functions of `domain/`; `SeriesCard` and `GapRow` are presentational,
  props in, no port; the view sorts and places what it renders, as it
  calls `booksInOrder` today.
- D07 — the runs in plain Vitest; components and the view with the real
  i18n, asserting text, never tags or classes; S2's `it.skip.each` turned
  `it.each` and nothing else in the scenario files; guards' mutations
  run, reverted, listed in the PR body.
- D10 — PRD words: a tome is a `volumeNumber` in code, never `volume`
  alone in a new name; `book`, never `edition` for a `Book`; a test body
  as Given / When / Then; scope `frontend`.
- D11 — the books come in no promised order: the runs are read whatever
  the answer's order.
- U01 — the missing row on `bg`, its outline and its words in `muted`.
- U02 — a missing row's run in the `row title` step, *Manquant(s)* in
  `body`; the card's rows in `body`, their values in tabular figures.
- U03 — the card is the content's first block, right after the header's
  12; the editions' block 20 under it.
- U05 — the card has the box of the loading state's card of
  `FieldRowSkeleton`s, so nothing jumps when the answer lands.
- U06 — a card of field rows alone: the label at the left in `muted`, the
  value at the right in `text`, right-aligned, a `border` hairline between
  two rows; a row with no value has none. The kind chooses the words
  through the key, every value of the series' kind, `null` included,
  carrying its entry; the component picks an entry and never branches on
  the kind.
- U08 — every word from the `fr` catalogue, *à* included; plain spaces in
  *1 à 25*, no non-breaking space (only before `:`, `;`, `?`).

## Risks and decisions
**Two domain functions, one for the numbers, one for the list.**
`runsOf` answers the runs owned and missing as numbers, read by the card;
`booksAndGapsInOrder` answers what the list renders, a gap placed before
the edition of the tome after it, so a gap never follows an edition
without a tome. Both are pure and in `domain/`, where D05 puts series gaps.
Rejected: the view interleaving in a `computed` (a rule only a mounted
view could test); one function answering both (the card needs no book).

**A run is its two ends; its reading is the catalogue's.** `Run` is
`{ first, last }`, a run of two one run, so a gap of tomes 4 and 5 is one
row, *Tomes 4, 5* over *Manquants*, as the spec's "one row per run" reads.
The three readings, *4*, *4, 5*, *4 à 6*, are one entry of three forms,
which vue-i18n picks by `last - first` (0, 1, more): *à* is a French word
(U08) and the component picks without branching. Rejected: a run of two
split into two runs in the domain (two missing rows for one run); three
entries chosen by an `if` in each component.

**The card and the missing row are components of their own.** `SeriesCard`
takes the whole `SeriesWithBooks`, since T106 reads its books' authors and
publishers there; `GapRow` takes the run and the series' kind, the kind of
the series and not of an edition choosing the words, as series-list S2
counts. The card holds no list item: the view spec and S1 read the
editions' list items across the page. Rejected: the card's rows written
in the view (T106 would grow the view); `CatalogueRow` learning a missing
state (a row with no book).

**The box of the card.** Its rows are the field rows of `SourceEditionCard`
in a card divided like the loading one, so nothing jumps (U05), with no
hairline above the first row, as the mockup draws it. The dashed outline
is `CatalogueRow`'s cover block, empty. Checked on the Pixel with the
spec's *Done*.

**S2 un-skipped last, a guard**, as in T103 and T104: by step 18 it
passes. The fields' pattern of the scenario reads plain spaces only, so a
non-breaking space in `run` reds it.
