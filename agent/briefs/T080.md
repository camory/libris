## Goal
On the home page a reader opens a form as the last row of *Mes
bibliothèques*, names a bookshelf and creates it; the new row joins the list
in S1's order. T081 builds the refusals and the unavailable case on this form.

## Risks and decisions
**The created row comes from the creation's answer, not a second read.**
The composable answers the membership `{ bookshelf, role: "OWNER" }`, the
contract's "the reader its one owner", and the view lists it with the
listed ones through `membershipsInOrder`, keeping every bookshelf created on
the page. The spec's *Creating* state says "on the answer the new row is in
the list". Rejected: reading `GET /api/v1/bookshelves` again (a second
answer to wait for, and its failure would replace a list that did not
change by the loading sentence). Rejected: the view deciding the role (a
value the application computes, so tested there).

**The blank rule is the domain's.** U04 keeps the rule that reads a field
out of the field; `isBookshelfName` sits beside `Bookshelf`, as `Isbn.of`
beside the ISBN. It answers a boolean: the name is sent as typed, so there
is no value to build. Rejected: `name.trim()` in the component.

**The client answers all three responses now.** D07 tests every response
the contract declares on an operation the client implements; T081 reads
the `409` on screen. `problemOf` keeps a proxy's non-problem answer a
rejection, as for the other clients.

**The stroke is provided once, in `createLibrisApp`.** `@lucide/vue` reads
its defaults from the context `LUCIDE_CONTEXT` (`setLucideProps` is the same
`provide` from a component); `createLibrisApp` is where D05 wires the app.
No test: a stroke is drawing, like a class, checked against the mockups.
Rejected: `strokeWidth` on every icon (U07 says once); `setLucideProps` in
`App.vue` (wiring moves into the shell).

**The plus waits for the rows.** It shows when the list is listed, as the
spec's *Loading* and *Unavailable* states say, so the guard of step 14 is
S9's existing case. Rejected: a new view case for the unavailable plus (it
would repeat the scenario's assertion).

**`@lucide/vue` is imported from its root.** The package declares no
sub-path exports and `sideEffects: false`, so Vite keeps the two icons. If
Vitest's load of the barrel visibly slows the gate, record the measure in
the gotcha; do not deep-import from `dist`.
