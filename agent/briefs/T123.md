## Goal
The reader's edits, on the form of a lacking or an unknown ISBN, are what
the add sends, read by the domain; the card of what was sent then confirms
the add. The language's word goes back to its code, the rule T124's
*Langue inconnue* and T127's suggestions read.

## Risks and decisions
**The button belongs to the form.** `EditionForm` draws `DockedAdd`,
takes `adding` and `failed`, and on the tap emits `add` with the edition
its fields read as; T124 refuses and focuses on that tap, inside the form,
before emitting. The view draws `DockedAdd` itself under the held card.
The form's root becomes a plain block holding the `<form>` then the dock,
so the dock is sticky over the whole form and outside its border.
- Rejected: emitting the edition on each change (T124 refuses on the tap only).
- Rejected: `defineExpose` read by the view (not props in, events out).
- Rejected: a `form=` button in the view (jsdom resolves no owner on a detached mount).
- Rejected: a slot for the button (the form would not own the tap).

**The reading is `editionOf` in `domain/EditionDraft.ts`**, the form
holding strings as U04 says and the domain reading them. It reads a form
T124's checks will have passed: no case gives it a number that is not a
whole one, nor a word naming no language (`null` meanwhile).
- Rejected: reading in the component (a rule out of the domain).
- Rejected: a `parse` answering refusals now (T124's, no scenario here).

**`languageCode` beside `languageWord`**, matching the list's words under
the base collator; the list stays private until T127 suggests from it.
- Rejected: two-letter pairs through `Intl.DisplayNames` (190 words, withdrawn codes).
- Rejected: `toLowerCase` and `normalize("NFD")` (the collator is the tree's way).

**Fields and kinds `disabled` while adding**: they accept nothing, the
keyboard closes, and they look the same, preflight's `color: inherit`
beating the browser's grey; checked by hand on `7-adding.jpg`. The cover's
dots stay as on the card.
- Rejected: `readonly` (radios ignore it).
- Rejected: a `<fieldset disabled>` (it disables the dots too).

**After the add, the card of the edition sent**, built by the view from
what it sent, the answer's `held` (`false`) and the sent cover alone. Its
cover column therefore draws one dot and the source's name, where
`8-added.jpg` draws neither: covers S3 reads *Open Library* on the screen
after the add, and its scenario is not edited. Flagged for Tophe in the PR
body.
- Rejected: `held` true on the card (covers S3 red).
- Rejected: keeping the form, the button gone (S5 reads the card).
