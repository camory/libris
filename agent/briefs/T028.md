# T028 — The new version and its banner

**Backlog line:** Frontend: the new version and its banner. Precondition
(human): `frontend/src/scenario/UpdateScenarios.spec.ts`, one skipped test per
scenario it realises, bearing the scenario's exact title, over a stubbed
registration (D07). `application`: the `AppUpdate` port and its injection key,
which announces that a newer version is waiting and takes the reader's order to
install it (D05). `infra/pwa`: the adapter over `navigator.serviceWorker` — it
registers the worker, announces the one already waiting when the app starts and
the one that becomes waiting while it runs, tells the waiting worker to take
over on the order and reloads the app once when the new worker takes control;
nothing at all where the browser has no service worker. `vite.config.ts` turns
the plugin from `autoUpdate` to `prompt` and injects no registration script of
its own, so a new worker waits for the reader instead of taking over on the
next load; `bootstrap` builds the adapter over the real registration and hands
it the reload, as it hands `FetchMeApi` the redirect, and `createLibrisApp`
provides it, `main.ts` still the only module reading `import.meta.env` (D05).
The adapter's spec stubs `navigator.serviceWorker` the way
`CameraBarcodeScanner`'s stubs `navigator.mediaDevices` and passes a fake
reload; jsdom seals `window.location` (D07). `ui/components`: the banner of
U09 — a refresh icon, the sentence *Nouvelle version disponible* and the text
button *Mettre à jour* at the right, every word from the `fr` catalogue (U04,
U07, U08); the shell shows it above the header of whatever screen is on, and
shows nothing while no version waits. The tap gives the order: the banner reads
*Mise à jour…* with a spinner in place of the icon and no button, until the
reload. Nothing reloads before the tap and the screen keeps what it shows.
Component tests over the three states of the banner. Realises S1, S2 and S4;
un-skips `S1 A new version is ready`, `S2 The reader updates` and `S4 Nothing
new`.

**Serves:** spec `update` — S1 *A new version is ready*, S2 *The reader
updates*, S4 *Nothing new*, and the *Screen* section ("No route of its own: the
banner of U09 above the header of every screen, owned by the app shell", the
three states *Ready*, *Updating*, *Current*); PRD §4.8 (the app installed on an
Android home screen) and §3 for the words; decisions D05, D07, D08, D10, and
D04 by its silence — the phase header says "Contract: none, the feature is

**First of its kind:** the first **thing the app says on its own**, and with it
three conventions the tree has none of yet. It is the first chrome the app
shell drives from an application port: `App.vue` injects `appUpdateKey`, and
`eslint.config.ts` refuses that import today — `ui-shell` may reach
`ui-components` and nothing else. The task adds one `boundaries/files`
category, `shell` on `src/ui/App.vue` alone, with a policy to `application`,
the way the `router` category already gives `src/ui/router.ts` the one import
its element type does not have; `i18n.ts` and `style.css`, `ui-shell` too, stay
refused. D05's numbered rules say what `ui/components` and `ui/views` may
import and are silent on the shell, so this widens no written rule, but it
widens the enforcement invented in T006: **the pull request body proposes the
amendment to D05 rule 4** ("…and the app shell may inject the ports of the
chrome it owns"), per `CLAUDE.md`, and does not edit `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`. It
is also the first `infra/pwa` package, the first adapter over a browser
registration rather than over `fetch` or the camera, and the first banner of
U09 — the first text button of U04 anywhere in the tree. The reviewer repeats
this line in its verdict and Tophe reviews the pull request interactively
before merging.

## Goal
When this task is done, a version deployed while the app is open announces
itself in one row above the screen, *Nouvelle version disponible* with *Mettre
à jour* at its right, the screen keeping every word it was showing; the tap
turns the row into *Mise à jour…* with a spinner, tells the worker that waits
to take over, and the app comes back once on the new version. It matters now
because every merge is a deploy: today `registerType: "autoUpdate"` makes a new
worker take over on the next load, so the reader sees the previous revision
until they relaunch the app twice (`agent/GOTCHAS.md`), and the revision at the
foot of the home page — the only thing that tells Tophe which version he is
looking at — lies for one launch.

## Rules in play
- **D04** — no contract, no pin. The feature is between the app and its own
  static server; `frontend/src/infra/api` is not touched and
  `frontend/api/openapi.yaml`'s pin does not move.
- **D05** — the `AppUpdate` port is a plain interface with an `InjectionKey`
  beside it in `src/application`, like `BarcodeScanner`; `infra/pwa` implements
  it over `navigator.serviceWorker`, may import `domain` and `application` and
  never `ui`; `ui/components` is presentational, props in and events out, so
  the banner injects nothing and holds no port. Wiring happens once in
  `createLibrisApp`, through provide/inject with a typed key. `main.ts` stays
  the only module that reads `import.meta.env`; `bootstrap` is the only module
  that reads `window`, and it hands the adapter the reload as it hands
  `FetchMeApi` the redirect. PWA via `vite-plugin-pwa` (Workbox) — the plugin
  changes mode, no PWA dependency is added or removed.
- **D07** — `cd frontend && npm test` is the gate, run plainly as the last
  command of its call. The three cases of
  `src/scenario/UpdateScenarios.spec.ts` are un-skipped and **nothing else in
  that file changes**; the harness it already holds (the stubbed container, the
  registration, `aNewerVersionIsFound`) is the contract the adapter must
  satisfy — read it before writing a line of the adapter. `infra` is proven by
  its own spec over a stubbed global, like `CameraBarcodeScanner.spec.ts`; a
  component test mounts with props and asserts the rendered text, the roles and
  the emitted events, never a tag, a class, a colour or a pixel; every body is
  `// Given`, `// When`, `// Then` unless it is a single statement; the spec
  sits beside the code it exercises and the shared fake goes to `src/fixture`.
- **D08** — nothing is installed: the adapter needs no package, the spec needs
  no polyfill, and `contracteer mock` keeps starting from the global setup for
  the `infra/api` specs.
- **D10** — TypeScript strict, ESLint with the boundaries rules, Prettier; code
  and identifiers in English, the screen's words in French; Conventional
  Commits with the scope `frontend`, branch `task/T028-update-banner`, the
  trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` on every commit. No
  rationale in code: no comment and no configuration key explaining *why*
  `prompt` replaces `autoUpdate`, and no `D##` or `U##` anywhere under
  `frontend/`.
- **U01** — the banner is `bg-surface` with a `border` hairline below, the icon
  and the button `accent`, the sentence `text`. Role names only: no slate, teal
  or red shade in the template, and no new colour role — the ones in
  `style.css` cover this task.
- **U02** — the sentence takes the `body` step (`text-body`), the button the
  `button` step (`text-button`). Both exist in `src/ui/style.css`; no step is
  added and no template writes a size.
- **U03** — the column is capped at 480 and centred, so the banner's row is
  capped and centred like the tab bar's, the surface and the hairline spanning
  the width. Spacing: 8 between the icon and its sentence.
- **U04**, *Text button* — a verb in the infinitive, `button` step, `accent`,
  no border, no background, 44 tall, 12 of side padding: *Mettre à jour*. It is
  a `<button>`, not a link; the screen's own primary button, *Chercher*, is
  untouched.
- **U05** — the spinner of the busy state is the one the primary button already
  turns, in `accent` here instead of white; no message, no skeleton, no empty
  state is involved.
- **U07** — `IconRefresh`, a new inline SVG under `ui/components/icons`, 22 on
  the 24 grid, stroke 1.8, round caps and joins, `currentColor`, no fill,
  drawn like `IconBarcode.vue` beside it. It sits next to a sentence, so it is
  decoration: `aria-hidden="true"`, no spec of its own.
- **U08** — *Nouvelle version disponible*, *Mettre à jour*, *Mise à jour…*,
  exactly as the spec writes them, from the `fr` catalogue: no French string in
  a component, no full stop on these three (a label of a few words), the
  ellipsis a single character `…` as `isbn.searching` has it.
- **U09** — the banner: `surface`, a `border` hairline below, 20 of padding at
  the left, 8 at the right, 5 above and below; the icon 22 in `accent`, one
  sentence in `body`/`text` 8 apart, the text button at the right. It is part
  of the page — it pushes the screen down, floats over nothing — one at a time,
  owned by the app shell, not by a screen, and it stays until the reader acts.
  While its action runs the sentence becomes the action in progress with an
  ellipsis, a spinner takes the icon's place and the button leaves.

## Out of scope
- **S3, the check schedule** — the hourly check and the one on coming back to
  the foreground. That is T029, the next line of the phase: no
  `registration.update()`, no timer, no `visibilitychange` listener in this
  task. The adapter answers the signals the browser sends it and nothing more.
- **A generic banner for anything else the app might say.** U09 says one banner
  at a time; the app has one thing to say. A second sentence is a second spec.
- **Dismissing the banner**, snoozing it, remembering a refusal. U09: it stays
  until the reader acts, and no document gives a way to refuse.
- **Offline behaviour, precaching strategy, the runtime caches of D05.** The
  Workbox options keep the values they have; only the registration mode
  changes.
- **`virtual:pwa-register` and its `useRegisterSW` composable.** The task line
  says the plugin injects no registration script of its own and the adapter
  registers over `navigator.serviceWorker`; the virtual module would put
  `vite-plugin-pwa` inside `infra` *and* make the type check depend on a plugin
  virtual type. If the implementer believes it is the better shape, that is a
  line in the pull request body, not a change of plan mid-run.
- **The `deploy/compose.yaml` and the release pipeline.** Nothing about how the
  new version reaches the server changes.
- **Redrawing any screen.** The banner pushes what is under it down; no view,
  no padding, no title moves.

## Risks and decisions
- **The shell needs the port, and `eslint.config.ts` refuses it today —
  decided: one `boundaries/files` category, `shell`, on `src/ui/App.vue`.**
  Probed on this tree: `import { meApiKey } from "../application/MeApi"` in
  `App.vue` fails `npx eslint` with *There is no policy allowing dependencies
  from elements of type "ui-shell" to elements of type "application"*, and a
  **type-only** import fails identically; with the `shell` category and its
  policy, `App.vue` passes and `i18n.ts` still fails. The alternative — passing
  the port as a root prop from `createLibrisApp` — duplicates the port's type
  in the shell and contradicts the task line ("`createLibrisApp` provides it")
  and D05 ("wiring happens once, in `createLibrisApp`, through provide/inject
  with typed keys"). The category is the narrow form: it widens one file, not
  the type, exactly as the `router` category does for `src/ui/router.ts`. See
  *First of its kind*: the pull request body proposes the D05 wording.
- **The banner is the first child of the shell's column, above the scrolling
  part — decided here.** U09 says it is part of the page, pushes the screen
  down and floats over nothing, which rules out a fixed or absolute overlay;
  U03 says the header scrolls with its screen while the shell's chrome stays
  put, and "stays until the reader acts" reads as staying visible. So the
  screen scrolls under it and the banner holds its 44-and-change of height at
  the top for as long as it is there.
- **The banner takes its state as one prop of three values, `none`, `ready`,
  `updating` — decided here**, rather than a boolean plus a `v-if` in the
  shell. The task line asks for "component tests over the three states of the
  banner": a state that lives in the shell's `v-if` cannot be one of them.
  `none` renders nothing, which is what U09's *Current* state is.
- **The two refs live in `App.vue`, with no composable — decided here.** The
  tree has no composable yet: `IsbnView` holds its own state and calls its
  port directly, and this is two lines of it. `application/` gets the port, not
  a use case; the day a second screen needs the same state it becomes one.
- **The port's words are `onNewVersion` and `install` — decided here**, from
  the spec's own sentence ("a new version is ready", "the reader taps the
  action") and PRD §3. `onNewVersion` announces at once if a version is already
  waiting when the listener arrives, so the shell may subscribe before or after
  the registration resolves; that ordering is otherwise a race between
  `bootstrap` and `mount`, and the scenario file gives no guarantee about it.
- **The catalogue keys are `update.available`, `update.install` and
  `update.installing` — decided here**, one section per screen or piece of
  chrome, as `home`, `isbn`, `tabs`, `language` and `role` already are; the
  `install`/`installing` pair mirrors `isbn.search`/`isbn.searching`.
- **The adapter is named `ServiceWorkerAppUpdate` and lives in `infra/pwa` —
  decided here**: the technology then the port, as `FetchMeApi`,
  `FetchIsbnApi` and `CameraBarcodeScanner` are named, and `infra/pwa` is the
  folder the task line names.
- **`injectRegister: false` and the app registering `/sw.js` itself — decided
  here.** `vite-plugin-pwa` defaults `injectRegister` to `auto`, which writes a
  `registerSW.js` script into `index.html`; the task line says the plugin
  injects no registration script of its own, and two registrations of the same
  worker would race for the announcement. `prompt` is what leaves
  `workbox.skipWaiting` false, and Workbox only writes the `SKIP_WAITING`
  message listener into `sw.js` when it is false — the order the adapter sends
  has a receiver only in this mode. The file name `sw.js` is the plugin's
  default and the path `nginx.conf` already serves with `no-cache`.
- **`nginx.conf` loses its `/registerSW.js` line — decided here.** The block
  serves a file that stops existing in this very task, and `CLAUDE.md` asks for
  only what the task uses. It is three words in a file no test reads; the
  `sw.js` and `index.html` no-cache lines above it are what keeps the update
  flow working and stay exactly as they are.
- **The first-install case has no scenario, and is kept.** The scenarios' fake
  container always has a controller, so nothing there would catch a banner
  shown to a reader who just installed the app for the first time. It is one
  `if` and one case in the adapter's spec.
- **The risk to watch on the Pixel, for the pull request body and the spec's
  *Done*:** a worker that is already waiting when the app is launched (the
  reader closed the app on the banner and opened it again) must announce
  itself, and the reload after the order must happen exactly once — a second
  reload loop is the classic failure of this flow. Both have their case here;
  the phone is what confirms them, and the *Done* of `specs/update.md` is
  Tophe's, not the run's.
