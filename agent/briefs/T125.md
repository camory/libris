## Risks and decisions
**The kind's word lives in the key, for the label and the refusal.**
`isbn.form.volume.<KIND>` and `isbn.form.refusal.withoutSeries.<KIND>`,
each kind written out, as `isbn.card.series` and `role` are; the form
builds the key with the chosen kind and never tests its value. The code
the domain answers stays kind-free: the rule is the same for every kind,
only its words differ.
- Rejected: `"{volume} sans série"` filled with the label (a message built from a label; the choice is the key's).
- Rejected: every refusal keyed by kind (three copies of three words no kind changes).
- Rejected: a code per kind from the domain (the domain would hold the screen's words).

**A number that is not one is refused as such first.** A volume `"0"`
with no series answers `invalidNumber`: `0` is no volume number, so it
cannot be one without a series; one code per field, as `Refusals` holds.
- Rejected: `withoutSeries` first (the reader adds a series and is told next tap that the number is wrong).

**A series of spaces alone is no series**, as `editionOf` reads it, which
drops the volume of such a draft: the refusal is what keeps a typed
number from vanishing on the add.
- Rejected: the series read untrimmed (a series of spaces alone would let the volume be dropped silently).

**A refusal's words follow the chosen kind until the next tap.** The form
keeps the codes of the last tap, as T124 decided, and words them in the
kind chosen now: a refused *Tome* turned *Album* by the kind reads *Album
sans série*. The refusal itself changes only on the next tap.
- Rejected: the words fixed at the tap (*Tome sans série* under *Album*).

**The authors' roles read the chosen kind**: `AuthorLines` takes the form's
`kind` in place of `edition.kind`, its props unchanged.
- Rejected: a role word chosen in the form (a second wording of the roles beside the card's).
