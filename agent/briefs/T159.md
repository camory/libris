## Goal
The frontend reads contract `v0.18.0`, the application holds the editions'
port with its client over `PUT /api/v1/books/{id}`, and the lookup offers
its pencil on the card of an edition the house holds that none of the
reader's copies carries, the form showing the house's cover without dots.
T160 to T163 then bring the pencil to the edition page and save through the
port.

## Risks and decisions
**The deploy precondition is Tophe's.** As T121 and T144 say, no run can
see what is deployed: this brief is written on T158 deployed, Tophe
launching the loop. The PR body says merging is not deploying.

**The pencil follows the reader's copies, not the house alone.** S1 shows
it on a held card with none of the reader's copies and hides it when one is
there; a card the house lacks keeps it. The removal case of step 7 follows:
once the added copy is removed the card is the house's with none of the
reader's copies, so it carries the pencil. *Offers no pencil on the card it
added* stays as it is, that card having no corner.
Rejected: keeping the removal case's "no pencil" (contradicts S1).

**`held` is a required prop of the form.** The form's cover follows the
card's rule, and both callers of `EditionForm` know whether the house holds
the edition; a required prop makes `vue-tsc` name each caller. The spec's
`form()` gives false by default so no case changes.
Rejected: an optional prop (a caller forgetting it draws dots on the
house's cover).

**The client's cases use the contract's body, not `onePiece2`.** The mock
answers its `404` only to the exact example body, so the spec writes that
body; the fixture keeps its own values for the views.
Rejected: changing `onePiece2` (the scenarios read it).
