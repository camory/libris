## Goal
The member who opens a bookshelf reads their own row first, and an owner
chooses the reader to add in the block *Choisir un lecteur*, no native
`select` left. T088 then puts the add's failure under the chosen name and
the sentence for every reader a member.

## Rules in play
- D05 — the order is the domain's (`membersInOrder`); `ReaderChoice` is a
  component, props in and events out, importing `domain` alone;
  `BookshelfView` wires it to `useAddMemberToBookshelf`.
- D07 — component and view specs assert text, roles, `pressed`, `disabled`
  and emitted events, never tags or classes; the three scenario runs
  un-skipped, their bodies unchanged; each guard's mutation run, reverted,
  listed in the PR body.
- D10 — PRD words (`reader`, `readerId`, `member`); the file reads top
  down; no rationale and no `D##`/`U##` in code.
- U03 — the plus right of *Membres* opens the block under the members'
  block, 20 apart; the plus is gone while the block is there and back when
  it closes or the add succeeds.
- U04 — the block: its label *Choisir un lecteur* in the `label` step,
  `muted`, at its right the icon button *Fermer*, Lucide `X` in `muted`;
  under it a card (`surface`, 1 px `border`, radius 14) holding the list,
  one row per reader in `body`, `text`, the whole row a button. Every row
  56 tall (the form's 44 buttons and 6 above and below), so the list shows
  five rows and half of the sixth, `max-h-[313px]` with its hairlines, and
  scrolls inside itself. The chosen row: the name, the crown, then the
  check at stroke 3 or the spinner while sent. While sent, every other
  button of the block is disabled, nothing dimmed.
- U07 — `X`, `Crown` (filled with `currentColor` when on), `Check`, from
  `@lucide/vue`; `IconClose` goes, no hand-drawn icon is added.
- U08 — every word in the `fr` catalogue.

## Risks and decisions
**`membersInOrder` takes the reader's id**, between the members and the
locale, and puts that member first whatever their role; the other members
keep T082's order. The view already holds the reader before it computes
the list.
- Rejected: moving the reader in the view — the order is the domain's,
  tested once.

**One component, `ReaderChoice`, is the whole block**: label, cross, list
and form, the chosen reader and the crown held inside it as
`NewMemberRow` held them; the view only shows it, hands it the sorted
readers and `adding`, and hears `add` and `close`. Closing unmounts it,
so a reopened block has nothing chosen.
- Rejected: the chosen reader held by the view — state no other part of
  the screen reads.
- Rejected: a generic choice component — one use; the second makes it.

**The chosen row's name is text, no longer a button**: the row is the form
(U04), and a tap on the chosen name has nothing to do.

**While adding, every other button of the block is disabled**: the other
readers, the crown and the cross, which is what "accepts nothing else"
means; nothing dims, as mockup 15 draws it.

**The block brings itself into view when mounted**, `block: "nearest"`, so
the page scrolls only as far as the block needs.
- Rejected: the view calling it — the view would reach into the
  component's element.

**The rows' height and the list's cap are classes no test sees**: jsdom
lays nothing out, so five rows and a half (56 each, 313 px with the
hairlines) is read by the reviewer and checked on the phone.

**The camera's `X` keeps `IconClose`'s 22**, the size of the barcode it
toggles with; the block's `X` takes Lucide's 24, as `CirclePlus`.
