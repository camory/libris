## Goal
The add sends every author's name without the spaces typed at its ends,
and sends nothing for a box whose name is empty or blank, whatever its
roles, without a word on the screen. The house then never receives a
blank author or a name padded with spaces from the form.

## Risks and decisions
**The rule lives in `editionOf`, not in the form.** `editionOf` already
reads every text of the draft through the trim (`text`), and already
turns the boxes into the authors sent (`sourceAuthorsOf`); the names
follow the same door. The form keeps handing over the boxes as typed.
Rejected: trimming in `EditionForm`'s `add()` (a rule in a component,
D05); trimming on input in `AuthorBox` (U04: what the reader types stays
as typed).

**A "space at the ends" is what `String.prototype.trim` removes.** The
draft's other texts are trimmed that way, title included, so one rule
holds for every text the form sends. The backend's matching trims the
space character alone (T143), and keeps the name as sent: a name trimmed
wider here only sends less. Rejected: trimming `' '` alone (two rules for
the texts of one form).

**No refusal, no form change, for a blank box.** S9 says no message
shows: the drop happens where the authors are sent, after `refusalsOf`
has answered none, which does not read the authors yet.
