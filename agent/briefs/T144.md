## Goal
The frontend reads contract `v0.17.0`, the application holds the authors'
port with its client, and the edition form opens each author of the card
as a box: its name, then its roles in the chosen kind's words. T145 to
T152 make the boxes add, remove, choose, suggest and refuse.

## Risks and decisions
**The deploy precondition is Tophe's.** As T087, T103, T121 and T132 say,
no run can see what is deployed: this brief is written on T143 deployed,
Tophe launching the loop. The PR body says merging is not deploying.

**One component, `AuthorBox`, for a box of lines.** The box is one
author's name and roles, and T145 to T152 add the trash, the word, the
roles' list and the refusal to it. Inside `EditionForm`, already about 330
lines, those would bury the form. Its props are `roles` (`AuthorRole[]`)
and `kind`; the name is its model, `defineModel` under `name`. Rejected:
the block inline in `EditionForm` (every later task grows the biggest
file); roles as a model now (no task step changes them before T147).

**The name is a model, held by the form.** Measured on a throwaway
component: a field bound one way loses the reader's text when its parent
redraws, as a change of kind does. Step 15 reds on that, and the form holds
its authors from `authorsInOrder` once, as it holds its field values.
Rejected: `:value` alone (step 15).

**The add still sends the answer's authors.** Sending the boxes, edits
included, is T145's with its scenarios; a name typed here is held, not sent.

**Helpers, not expectations.** `valuesOf`, `disabledOf` and `suggestions`
would count the boxes; leaving them out keeps every case reading what it
read. The boxes' disabled state is T146's and T147's, with their cases.

**Words.** *Nom de l'auteur* and *Rôles* go beside *Auteurs*, which is
`isbn.form.authors` today. The implementer may turn that key into a block
(`isbn.form.authors.label`, `.name`, `.roles`), the shape later tasks'
words (*Ajouter un auteur*, *Retirer l'auteur*) join. The role words stay
`role.<kind>.<role>`. The roles' `aria-label` is "Rôles" alone when empty
and "Rôles " followed by the line's text otherwise. Rejected: words written
in `AuthorBox` (U08).

**The chevron is drawn, never turned:** the list opens with T147 only, so
`aria-expanded` stays `"false"` and the chevron down.
