## Risks and decisions
**The layout is proven by commands, not by a view test.** jsdom lays nothing
out, and D07 forbids asserting a class or a tag in a view test, so the square,
the radius, the colour, the pressed shade and the 8 are read by the greps and
the diff of the criteria, as T041 proved its weight, and by Tophe on the
Pixel. The view cases prove what the reader perceives without layout: no
word, the glyph, the spinner in its place, the names.
- Rejected: asserting `classes()` or a parent element in a view test — a
  class or a structure in a test.

**The glyph is 22 and the spinner takes its size.** U07 names no size for a
glyph in a button; 22 is the default of every icon of the family and the size
the scanner's barcode is drawn at inside the same field, so the component is
used as is, with no class. U04 puts the spinner *in the place of the glyph*,
so it is drawn at the glyph's 22 (`size-5.5`) and nothing moves when it
replaces it; the add button's spinner, beside a word, keeps its 16.
- Rejected: the tab bar's 32 — U07 gives it to the tab bar alone, and it
  would crowd a 50 square.

**The box is written `size-[50px]`,** as the field's `h-[50px]`: GOTCHAS
reads the box of a control as a length written as is, not a token, and the
two controls of one line then say the same 50.
- Rejected: `size-12.5` — the same length, in a second spelling beside the
  field's.

**The icon is `IconMagnifier`**, named by what it shows, as U07 names its
family (`IconBarcode`, not `IconScan`).
- Rejected: `IconSearch` — the action, not the drawing.

**The words stay where they are.** *Chercher* and *Recherche en cours…* are
the catalogue's `isbn.search` and `isbn.searching`, now read into the
`aria-label`; the entries keep their keys and their text.

**PROPOSED's primary button item is rewritten, not removed.** After this task
the field's button and the card's share colour, radius, pressed shade, dimming
and spinner, but not their box or content; the item says so, and still waits
for a third primary button.
