page and of a bookshelf, the star of a home row, the check of a new
bookshelf, and the cross, the crown and the check of the choice of a

## Risks and decisions
**The cross *Fermer* takes `text-muted` on its icon, not on the tag.**
`IconButton`'s root says `text-accent`; a `text-muted` falling through
would sit beside it on one element, where Tailwind's emit order, not the
attribute, picks the colour (gotcha on two utilities for one property),
while a colour on the icon is its own declaration and beats the inherited
one. `BookshelvesHolding`'s *Annuler* already does so. Rejected: the class
on the tag (the winner set by an order nobody reads); a colour prop on
`IconButton` (an API for one caller).

**The hand-written buttons gain `shrink-0`.** `IconButton` holds it, four
of the seven did not (`HomeView`, `BookshelfView`, `NewBookshelfRow`,
*Fermer*); each sits beside a short label or inside a box of its own size,
so nothing is drawn differently, and the look on the running app goes to
Tophe. Rejected: a prop or a variant to leave it out (a difference no
screen shows).

**Five moves, five commits.** One commit per file keeps each diff a few
lines, its proof the specs of its row. Rejected: one commit for every move
(a red could not be traced to its file); seven commits, one per button
(`ReaderChoice`'s three share one spec and one import).
