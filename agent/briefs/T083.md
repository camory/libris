## Goal
An owner taps the plus beside *Membres*, chooses a reader who is not a
member, as *invité* or with the crown as *propriétaire*, and sees them among
the members. Refusals, no answer and every reader a member are T088's.

## Risks and decisions
**The readers are asked by `useFindBookshelf`, for an owner only.** The
spec asks them with the bookshelf, for an owner, and T088 reads their
failure as the bookshelf's: one state holds both. `find` takes the reader's
id and asks `readersApi.known()` once the answer names that reader `OWNER`;
the view calls `find` once the reader is known.
- Rejected: asking for anyone, in parallel — a viewer's screen would wait on a call the spec does not make.
- Rejected: a composable of their own — two states for one load, T088 merging them.

**The plus shows while someone can be added.** `nonMembersInOrder` over the
readers and every member, the added included, decides it; a viewer's empty
`readers` hides it. T088 adds the sentence for an owner.
- Rejected: an ownership check in the view — no case of T083 tells it apart.

**The added members live in the composable**, `added`, merged with the
found members before `membersInOrder`, as `useCreateBookshelf`'s `created`;
the view closes the form on `added`.
- Rejected: writing into `useFindBookshelf`'s state — one composable writing another's.

**The row receives the readers sorted**: the view hands it
`nonMembersInOrder(…)`.
- Rejected: sorting in the component — the order is the domain's, tested once.

**The placeholder is the select's first option**, value empty, selected,
in `muted` while chosen; the `select`'s `aria-label` is *Lecteur*, the
spec's name of the choice.
- Rejected: a visible label — U04 has none in a form in a list.

**The port joins `LibrisPorts` before the view injects it**, as T079's
step 3, so the scenario's `open()` wires what `bootstrap` wires.
- Rejected: keeping `app.provide` beside `createLibrisApp` — the task line moves it.
