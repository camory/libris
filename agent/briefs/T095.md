# T095 — The cover of a series

**Backlog line:**
> - [ ] T095 Backend: the cover of a series.
>       Each series of T093 answers the cover of its first edition in the
>       order a shelf reads, catalogue S1's: by tome, an edition without a
>       tome after the numbered tomes, then by title; its address as a
>       `Book`'s, or none when that edition has none, another edition's
>       never standing in (D11, a pick by position). Unit tests of the cover
>       rule, one per case: the first tome has a cover; the first tome has
>       none and the second has one, answered none; no edition has a tome,
>       the first by title giving the cover; S3's first red, the spec giving
>       it no backend scenario test. Realises S3.

**Serves:** spec `series-list` S3; spec `catalogue` S1 (the order a shelf
reads); PRD §3 (*Series*, *Edition*); decisions D02, D07, D10, D11, D12
**First of its kind:** the first order of text the backend's Kotlin
computes: within a series, titles compared ignoring case and accents
through `java.text.Collator`, where every order of names so far is the
database's collation or the screen's `Intl.Collator`.

## Goal
`ListCatalogueSeries` answers each series with the `coverName` of its first
edition in the order a shelf reads, or `null` when that edition has none,
where T094 left it `null`. With T093's count and T094's kind, the backend's
answer is whole and T096 can start once it is deployed.

## Test plan
Suite: `cd backend && ./gradlew check`. Each step one case, one cycle, one
commit, in this order; `./gradlew detekt` before each commit. Every case
sits in `ListCatalogueSeriesTest`, gives Léa one bookshelf (the class's
`bookshelfOf` helper) holding one copy (`copyOn`) of each edition named,
and asserts the whole answer, `series shouldBe
listOf(CatalogueSeries(name, count, cover, kind))`. Each green is the
smallest that passes the cases so far, as T094's were, since the next red
depends on it. `EditionsInMemory.findByIds` answers editions in the order
they were inserted: where a step names an insertion order, the class's
`editions` setup inserts them so.

1. **Red** (`ListCatalogueSeries`). `a series has the cover of its first
   tome`: `Thorgal`, BD, *La magicienne trahie* tome 1 with a cover,
   *L'île des mers gelées* tome 2 without; answered
   `CatalogueSeries("Thorgal", 2, <tome 1's cover>, BD)`. Red: `null`.
   Green, the smallest: the cover of the first of its editions that has
   one.
2. **Red** (`ListCatalogueSeries`). `a series whose first tome has no
   cover has none, though the second has one`: `Blake et Mortimer`, BD,
   *Le secret de l'Espadon* tome 1 without a cover, *Le mystère de la
   Grande Pyramide* tome 2 with one; answered
   `CatalogueSeries("Blake et Mortimer", 2, null, BD)`. Red: step 1
   answers tome 2's. Green, the smallest: the cover, or none, of the
   edition with the lowest tome, Kotlin's default order of a nullable key
   (`null` first) left as it is.
3. **Red** (`ListCatalogueSeries`). `an edition without a tome comes after
   the numbered tomes`: `Spirou et Fantasio`, BD, *Le journal d'un
   ingénu* without a tome and *4 aventures de Spirou et Fantasio* tome 1,
   each with a cover of its own; answered tome 1's. Red: step 2 puts the
   `null` tome first and answers the untomed edition's. Green: the tome
   key with `null` last.
4. **Red** (`ListCatalogueSeries`). `a series without tomes has the cover
   of its first edition by title`: `Les Cités obscures`, BD, *Les
   murailles de Samaris* inserted before *La fièvre d'Urbicande*, neither
   with a tome, each with a cover of its own; answered *La fièvre
   d'Urbicande*'s. Red: step 3 keeps the first of equal tomes, *Les
   murailles*. Green, the smallest: then by title, as `String` compares.
5. **Red** (`ListCatalogueSeries`). `titles are compared ignoring case and
   accents`: `Les Cités obscures`, BD, *L'Ombre d'un homme* and *L'écho
   des cités*, neither with a tome, each with a cover of its own; answered
   *L'écho des cités*'s. Red: `String` order puts `O` (and `o`) before
   `é`. Green: the title key through a `java.text.Collator` of
   `Locale.ROOT` at `Collator.PRIMARY`. Measured with jshell on JDK 25:
   the collator answers *L'écho* first, `String.compareTo` and a
   lowercased or an accent-stripped comparison all answer *L'Ombre*.
6. Tidy, `./gradlew detekt`, rewrite the gotcha, gate plainly, tick T095,
   PROGRESS entry, PR.

Not to be written: a case of two editions sharing a tome and a title (no
spec case; see *Risks*); a case of a lone edition; a `BOOK` or `MANGA`
cover case; any case on the order of the answer; a domain, JDBC, web or

## Risks and decisions
**Fit.** One run: one use case changed, five unit cases, two code files and
one gotcha. Rejected: a split (each case is two fixtures over the same
read).

**The pick is the use case's, beside the count and the kind.** As T094
found for the kind, no aggregate holds a series' editions (a series is a
value of the edition, D12), so the read that groups them is where the pick
is seen and tested. Rejected: a `SeriesEditions` domain type now (count,
kind and pick would move into it with their tests, a refactor no case
asks for; the series page, which will hold a series' editions, is where
it earns its place); the order in SQL through `EditionRepository.findByIds`
(the spec asks a unit test of the cover rule; a repository promising an
order no other caller needs).

**Titles ignoring case and accents, through a root `Collator` at
`PRIMARY`.** The spec takes the order a shelf reads, catalogue S1's, and
the shelf orders titles under the `ignoring_case_and_accents` collation
(ICU `und`, level 1): a `String` order would show *L'Ombre d'un homme*'s
cover on a shelf that reads *L'écho des cités* first. `java.text.Collator`
is the JDK's order of text, needs no dependency, and the `application`
rule allows `java..`; its root locale matches the collation's `und`. It
departs from the collation on one point, already measured in the
gotchas: it ignores spaces, so two titles equal but for a space fall to
the next key. Rejected: plain `String.compareTo` (accents and capitals
break the shelf's order); a folding beside `BookshelfName`'s (a second
copy of a private function, made for sameness, no closer to the
collation); `BookshelfName`'s folding made public (D10,
and the library's bookshelf name is not an edition's title). This is the
*First of its kind* line: Tophe settles in review whether it becomes the
rule for any order the Kotlin computes on text.

**No key after the title.** The catalogue ends its order on the id so that
a page is total (D11); a pick between two editions of one series with the
same tome and the same title has no spec case, and the cover of either is
that shelf's first by its words. The pick takes the first the read answers
among them. Rejected: the id as a last key (production code no spec case
motivates).

**Five cases for three.** The spec names three cases; the backlog line's
rule has two clauses those three never red, an untomed edition after the
numbered tomes and the title's case and accents. Each gets its case, so no
part of the comparison stands without a test that made it. Rejected: the
untomed clause folded into case 1 (one step, two claims).

**Greens kept the smallest.** Step 1 answers the first cover found, step 2
the lowest tome with `null` first, step 4 `String` titles: each is wrong in
the way the next step reds, so all five steps are red before green and
none is a guard. Rejected: the full comparator at step 2 (steps 3 to 5
would pass on arrival, guards proving less than a red).

**Fixtures.** Real BD series and titles, all `BD`, each edition one copy on
Léa's own bookshelf: the copies counted once are T093's case's. *Le journal
d'un ingénu* is Émile Bravo's Spirou, published without a tome; *Les Cités
obscures* are numbered differently by every publisher, so a household
records them without one; *L'écho des cités* written with a lower-case
`é`, as sources often leave a capital unaccented or lowered.
